Planning a internal plastering or external rendering project in the UK requires accurately calculating the number of **25kg plaster bags** (such as **British Gypsum Gyproc Multi-Finish**, **Board Finish**, or **Hardwall undercoat**) to purchase.

Understanding standard **spreading rates**—where a single 25kg bag of Multi-Finish skim covers **10 square metres at a standard 2mm thickness**—prevents mid-job shortages and waste.

## ⚙️ Industry Plaster Spreading Rates & Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Gypsum Plaster Spreading Rates (per 25kg Bag)
- **Gyproc Multi-Finish Skim Plaster (2mm Coat)**: Covers **10.0 sq m per 25kg bag**.
- **Gyproc Board Finish Skim Plaster (2mm Coat)**: Covers **10.0 sq m per 25kg bag** (designed for plasterboard).
- **Gyproc Hardwall Undercoat Plaster (11mm Thickness)**: Covers **3.0 sq m per 25kg bag** (high-impact masonry undercoat).
- **Gyproc ToughCoat Undercoat Plaster (11mm Thickness)**: Covers **3.5 sq m per 25kg bag**.

### 2. The 2-Coat Plastering System
- **Undercoat Backing Plaster (11mm)**: Applied directly to raw masonry brickwork or blockwork to level the surface (Hardwall or Sand & Cement render).
- **Finish Skim Coat (2mm)**: Applied in 2 thin coats (1mm + 1mm) over dry plasterboard or undercoat plaster to create a smooth, paintable surface.

### 3. Mixing & Spillage Buffer
- Mixing plaster on-site in buckets generates small mixing spillage and Hawk/Trowel drops. Always add a **10% material buffer** when ordering 25kg bags.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Plastering Warnings

1. **Exceeding Gypsum Plaster Shelf Life**: Gypsum plaster absorbs ambient atmospheric moisture over time. Plaster bags have a strict **4-month shelf life** from the manufacture date printed on the bag. Using old plaster causes lumpy mixes and rapid 'flash setting' on the board.
2. **Plastering Over Dry Un-primed Plasterboard**: Always use PVA or SBR bonding agents (or dedicated Blue Grit primer) when skimming over high-suction old plaster or low-suction painted walls to ensure mechanical adhesion.
3. **Plastering Down to the Concrete Floor**: Leave a **50mm gap** between the bottom edge of plasterboard/skim coat and the concrete floor slab to prevent ground dampness from soaking up into the plaster (capillary rise).
