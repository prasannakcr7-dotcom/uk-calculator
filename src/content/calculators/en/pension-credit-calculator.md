**Pension Credit** is a vital tax-free benefit administered by the UK Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) to top up weekly income for low-income retirees who have reached State Pension age.

In addition to providing direct weekly cash top-ups, Pension Credit serves as a critical **gateway benefit**, unlocking thousands of pounds in additional support including **Winter Fuel Payments**, **100% Council Tax Reduction**, and **free dental care**.

## ⚙️ Statutory Guarantee Pension Credit Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Guarantee Credit Weekly Minimum Baseline Levels
Guarantee Credit tops up your weekly income to a statutory minimum baseline:
- **Single Pensioner**: Income topped up to **£218.15 per week** (£11,343.80/year).
- **Pensioner Couple**: Combined income topped up to **£332.95 per week** (£17,313.40/year).

### 2. Capital & Savings Tariff Income Rules
- **First £10,000 Savings Disregarded**: Savings, investments, or capital under **£10,000** are completely ignored.
- **Tariff Income above £10,000**: For every £500 (or part of £500) of savings above £10,000, DWP counts **£1.00 per week** as income.
- *Example*: Having £14,000 in savings adds £8.00/week in assumed tariff income.

### 3. Disability & Carer Premiums
- **Severe Disability Addition**: Extra **£81.50 per week** if receiving Attendance Allowance or PIP Daily Living.
- **Carer Addition**: Extra **£45.60 per week** if entitled to Carer's Allowance.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Pension Credit Warnings

1. **Assuming Owning a Home Makes You Ineligible**: Owning the home you live in does NOT disqualify you from Pension Credit. Your primary residence is completely excluded from the capital means test.
2. **Missing Out on Passported Gateway Benefits**: Even if you qualify for as little as **£1.00 per week** in Pension Credit, you unlock:
   - **Winter Fuel Payment** (£200 to £300/yr).
   - **100% Council Tax Reduction** (saving ~£1,800/yr).
   - **Free TV Licence** (if aged 75 or over).
   - **Free NHS Dental Treatment, Glasses, & Travel Costs**.
3. **Backdating Limit (3 Months)**: Pension Credit claims can be backdated for a maximum of **3 months** from the date of application.
