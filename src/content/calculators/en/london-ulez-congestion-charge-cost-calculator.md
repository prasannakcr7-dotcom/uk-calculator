Driving a car or commercial van in Greater London requires navigating two distinct Transport for London (TfL) charging zones: the **Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ)** and the **Central London Congestion Charge Zone**.

Non-compliant vehicles driven across all 32 London boroughs incur the **£12.50 daily ULEZ charge**. If driving into Central London during charging hours, the **£15.00 daily Congestion Charge** also applies—resulting in a combined **£27.50 daily toll** (£3,300+ per year for regular commuters).

## ⚙️ Statutory TfL Charging Zones & Euro Standards for 2026/27

### 1. The Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ)
- **Coverage**: Operates **24 hours a day, 365 days a year** across all 32 Greater London boroughs out to the M25 boundary (excluding Christmas Day).
- **Daily Charge**: **£12.50 per day** for non-compliant vehicles.
- **Exemption Standards**:
  - *Petrol Vehicles*: Must meet **Euro 4 standards** (generally petrol cars registered after **1 January 2006**).
  - *Diesel Vehicles*: Must meet **Euro 6 standards** (generally diesel cars registered after **1 September 2015**).
  - *Electric Vehicles (EVs)*: 100% zero-emission exempt.

### 2. The Central London Congestion Charge
- **Coverage**: Central London zone (bounded by the Inner Ring Road including Mayfair, Soho, the City, and Westminster).
- **Operating Hours**: **07:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday**, and **12:00 to 18:00 Saturday, Sunday, and Bank Holidays** (no charge between Christmas Day and New Year's Day bank holiday).
- **Daily Charge**: **£15.00 per day** for all cars entering the zone (electric EVs are subject to Cleaner Vehicle Discount rules).

### 3. TfL Auto Pay & Penalty Charge Notices (PCNs)
- **TfL Auto Pay**: Registering your vehicle on TfL Auto Pay is **free**. Cameras automatically scan your license plate and bill your bank account monthly, eliminating forgotten payment fines.
- **Penalty Charge Notice (PCN)**: Failing to pay the ULEZ or Congestion Charge by midnight on the third day following travel results in a **£180 PCN** (reduced to £90 if paid within 14 days).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & London Driving Warnings

1. **Believing ULEZ Operates Only Mon-Fri**: Unlike the Congestion Charge, the **ULEZ operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year**. Driving a non-compliant car across midnight incurs two separate £12.50 daily charges (£25.00).
2. **Forgetting Rental / Courtesy Car Auto Pay**: If you drive a hire car or garage courtesy car inside London, verify whether the rental agency includes ULEZ coverage. If not registered on your TfL Auto Pay account, you will receive PCN fines.
3. **Assuming Hybrid Cars are Automatically ULEZ Exempt**: Older Euro 5 hybrid diesel cars registered before September 2015 do NOT meet Euro 6 standards and MUST pay the £12.50 daily ULEZ charge.
