The **Armed Forces Pension Scheme 2015 (AFPS 15)** is one of the most generous public sector pension schemes in the UK. It is a **100% non-contributory defined benefit scheme** provided to regular serving personnel in the **British Army**, **Royal Navy**, **Royal Marines**, and **Royal Air Force (RAF)**.

Under AFPS 15, members build up pension benefits based on a **Career Average Revalued Earnings (CARE)** structure with a high accrual rate of **1/47th (2.1276%)** of pensionable base pay earned each year. In addition, serving personnel who reach the **20/40 Early Departure Payment (EDP)** milestone receive immediate monthly income and a tax-free lump sum upon leaving the military.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **CARE Pension Accrual Formula**:
  - `Annual Pension Accrued = Pensionable Base Pay × (1 / 47)`.
  - Revalued annually in line with Consumer Prices Index (CPI) inflation plus 0%.
- **0% Member Contribution Rate**: Serving military personnel pay **£0.00 in pension contributions** from their monthly salary; the scheme is 100% funded by the Ministry of Defence.
- **Early Departure Payment Scheme (EDP 15)**:
  - **Qualification Milestone**: Must complete at least **20 years of regular service** AND be **age 40 or older** upon discharge.
  - **EDP Annual Income**: Pays **34% of your deferred annual pension** (plus 0.85% for each extra year served over 20) as regular income from discharge until State Pension Age.
  - **EDP Tax-Free Lump Sum**: Pays an immediate tax-free lump sum equal to **2.25 times your deferred annual pension value**.
- **Normal Pension Age (NPA)**: Age 60 for serving personnel; State Pension Age (67/68) for deferred members who leave prior to age 60.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Leaving at 19 Years Service (Missing EDP by 1 Year)**: Leaving the military after 19 years and 11 months forfeits the EDP tax-free lump sum and monthly income stream worth over £100,000 across early retirement years.
- **Confusing AFPS 15 with AFPS 75 or AFPS 05**: AFPS 75 calculated pensions based on rank and final rank pay; AFPS 15 uses career average earnings across your entire service duration.
- **Overlooking McCloud Remedy Transfer Adjustments**: Service between 2015 and 2022 is subject to the McCloud remedy, allowing eligible personnel to choose between legacy scheme (AFPS 75/05) or AFPS 15 benefits for the remedy period.
