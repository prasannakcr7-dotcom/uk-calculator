In April 2018, the UK Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) was replaced in Wales by a devolved property transaction tax called **Land Transaction Tax (LTT)** (known in Welsh as *Treth Tramwyad Tir*). LTT is collected by the **Welsh Revenue Authority (WRA / Awdurdod Revenue Cymru)**.

For home buyers purchasing a primary main residence in Wales, LTT offers a generous **0% tax threshold on property values up to £225,000**. However, for buyers purchasing additional residential properties (such as buy-to-let investments or second holiday homes), Wales enforces a separate **Higher Residential LTT Rate Scale** starting at **4% on the first £180,000**.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Standard Main Residence LTT Rates (2026/27)**:
  - **£0 – £225,000**: **0%** (Tax-Free Threshold).
  - **£225,001 – £400,000**: **6.0%**.
  - **£400,001 – £750,000**: **7.5%**.
  - **£750,001 – £1,500,000**: **10.0%**.
  - **Over £1,500,000**: **12.0%**.
- **Higher Rates for Additional Residential Properties (Buy-to-Let / Second Homes)**:
  - **£0 – £180,000**: **4.0%**.
  - **£180,001 – £250,000**: **7.5%**.
  - **£250,001 – £400,000**: **9.0%**.
  - **£400,001 – £750,000**: **11.5%**.
  - **£750,001 – £1,500,000**: **14.0%**.
  - **Over £1,500,000**: **16.0%**.
- **First-Time Buyer Relief**: Unlike England, Wales does NOT offer a separate First-Time Buyer LTT relief because the standard 0% threshold was permanently raised to **£225,000** for all main residence buyers.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Confusing English SDLT Rules with Welsh LTT**: SDLT does not apply to property purchases in Wales; conveyancers must file return form LTT-1 with WRA.
- **Assuming First-Time Buyer Discount Exists in Wales**: First-time buyers in Wales pay standard LTT rates (paying £0 under £225k, but standard 6% above £225k).
- **Replacing Main Residence Refund Window**: If you buy a new main home in Wales before selling your old one, you pay higher LTT rates upfront, but can claim a refund if your previous main home sells within 36 months.
