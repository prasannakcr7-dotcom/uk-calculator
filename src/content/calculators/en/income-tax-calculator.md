Income Tax is the primary tax levied by the UK government on personal earnings, including employment wages, self-employment profits, pensions, and rental income.

Understanding how your **Personal Allowance** and marginal tax brackets operate across UK nations ensures complete transparency over your monthly paycheck.

## ⚙️ Statutory Income Tax Bands & Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Standard UK Income Tax Bands (England, Wales, Northern Ireland)
- **Personal Allowance (£0 to £12,570)**: **0% Tax**.
- **Basic Rate Band (£12,571 to £50,270)**: **20% Income Tax**.
- **Higher Rate Band (£50,271 to £125,140)**: **40% Income Tax**.
- **Additional Rate Band (Above £125,140)**: **45% Income Tax**.

### 2. Devolved Scottish Income Tax Bands (Scotland Residents)
- **Personal Allowance**: **0% Tax** (£0 to £12,570).
- **Starter Rate (£12,571 to £14,876)**: **19%**.
- **Basic Rate (£14,877 to £26,561)**: **20%**.
- **Intermediate Rate (£26,562 to £43,662)**: **21%**.
- **Higher Rate (£43,663 to £75,000)**: **42%**.
- **Advanced Rate (£75,001 to £125,140)**: **45%**.
- **Top Rate (Above £125,140)**: **48%**.

### 3. The £100,000 Personal Allowance Taper
If your adjusted net income exceeds **£100,000**, your Personal Allowance is reduced by **£1 for every £2** of income above £100,000. Your Personal Allowance drops to zero once income reaches **£125,140**, creating an effective **60% marginal tax rate** in this zone.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Tax Code Warnings

1. **Incorrect PAYE Tax Codes**: Check your monthly payslip for tax codes like `1257L` (standard allowance), `BR` (taxed at 20% without allowance), or `K` (taxed on benefits in kind). Incorrect codes cause long-term over-taxation or under-taxation.
2. **The 60% Tax Trap Between £100k and £125k**: Earnings in the £100k–£125k taper window face a severe 60% effective tax rate (40% tax + 20% lost allowance tax). Increasing workplace pension contributions via salary sacrifice drops income back to £100k, recovering 100% of your Personal Allowance.
3. **Omitting Marriage Allowance Savings**: If one spouse earns under £12,570 and the other is a basic rate taxpayer, transferring 10% of the unused Personal Allowance saves up to **£252 per year** in tax.
