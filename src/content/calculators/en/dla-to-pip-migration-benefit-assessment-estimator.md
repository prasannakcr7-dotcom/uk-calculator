The UK Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has migrated working-age claimants from Disability Living Allowance (DLA) onto **Personal Independence Payment (PIP)** (or Adult Disability Payment in Scotland).

Unlike DLA, PIP is awarded based on an independent health assessment measuring how your physical or mental health condition affects your ability to carry out **12 daily living and mobility activities**. PIP is tax-free, non-means-tested, and paid every 4 weeks.

## ⚙️ Statutory DWP PIP Rates & Point Thresholds for 2026/27

### 1. PIP Component Weekly Rates (2026/27)
PIP is split into two components—**Daily Living** and **Mobility**—with two payment rates each:
- **Daily Living Component**:
  - *Standard Rate (8 to 11 points)*: **£72.65 per week**.
  - *Enhanced Rate (12+ points)*: **£108.55 per week**.
- **Mobility Component**:
  - *Standard Rate (8 to 11 points)*: **£28.70 per week**.
  - *Enhanced Rate (12+ points)*: **£79.35 per week**.

### 2. The PIP Assessment Point Threshold Rules
- **Under 8 Points**: Score 0 to 7 points on a component = **Nil Award (£0.00)** for that component.
- **8 to 11 Points**: Qualifies for the **Standard Rate** of that component.
- **12 or More Points**: Qualifies for the **Enhanced Rate** of that component.
- *Maximum PIP Award*: Receiving Enhanced Daily Living (£108.55) + Enhanced Mobility (£79.35) pays **£187.90 per week (£751.60 every 4 weeks / £9,770.80 per year)**.

### 3. The Motability Scheme Qualification
- Claimants awarded the **Enhanced Rate Mobility Component (£79.35/week)** are legally entitled to join the **Motability Scheme**, exchanging their weekly mobility payment to lease a brand-new car, wheelchair-accessible vehicle (WAV), scooter, or powered wheelchair (includes insurance, servicing, and breakdown cover).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & PIP Assessment Warnings

1. **Describing Your Best Days Instead of Majority of Days**: PIP descriptors evaluate how your condition affects you for at least **50% of the days in a 12-month period**. When completing the PIP2 questionnaire or attending the face-to-face/telephone assessment, describe your average or bad days, not your best days.
2. **Forgetting the Reliability Test Criteria (SAFELY)**: Under DWP regulations, an activity counts as completed ONLY if you can do it:
  - **S**afely (without risk of injury).
  - **A**cceptable standard.
  - **R**epeatedly (as often as required).
  - **T**imely (in no more than twice the normal time).
  If you cannot complete an activity safely or repeatedly, you score points as being unable to complete it.
3. **Failing to Request a Mandatory Reconsideration (MR) Within 1 Month**: If you disagree with a DWP PIP decision letter (e.g. awarded 6 points instead of 8), you MUST submit a **Mandatory Reconsideration request within 1 month** of the decision date. Over 65% of PIP decision appeals succeed at Independent Tribunal.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<div class="faq-sec">
  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Does receiving PIP affect my other benefits or Universal Credit? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No. PIP is completely tax-free and non-means-tested. Receiving PIP does NOT reduce Universal Credit, Employment and Support Allowance (ESA), or Tax Credits. In fact, a PIP award often unlocks extra disability premiums and carer benefits.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is Adult Disability Payment (ADP) in Scotland? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>In Scotland, Social Security Scotland has replaced PIP with **Adult Disability Payment (ADP)**. ADP uses identical weekly payment rates and point descriptors as PIP, but removes private medical assessment providers in favor of a practitioner-led process.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Can a family member receive Carer's Allowance if I am awarded PIP? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Yes. If you are awarded the Daily Living Component of PIP (Standard or Enhanced rate), a family member or friend providing at least 35 hours of unpaid care per week can claim **Carer's Allowance (£81.90 per week)**.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How long are PIP benefit awards granted for before review? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>PIP awards are granted for fixed periods ranging from 2 years up to 10 years (or ongoing light-touch reviews for severe permanent conditions) before a review questionnaire is sent by the DWP.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
