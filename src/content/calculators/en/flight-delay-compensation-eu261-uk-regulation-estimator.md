If your flight departing from a UK airport (or arriving in the UK on a UK/EU airline) is delayed by **3 hours or more** upon arrival at your final destination, you are legally entitled to fixed statutory cash compensation under **UK261 Regulations** (the retained UK version of EU Regulation 261/2004).

Depending on the flight distance, statutory cash payouts range from **£220 to £520 (€250 to €600) per passenger**—regardless of the original ticket price!

## ⚙️ Statutory UK261 / EU261 Compensation Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Fixed Statutory Compensation Tiers per Passenger
Compensation is fixed based on flight distance and final arrival delay duration:
- **Short-Haul Flights (Under 1,500 km)**:
  - *Delay 3+ hours*: **£220.00 (€250)** per passenger.
  - *Example*: London to Paris, Amsterdam, Dublin, or Edinburgh.
- **Medium-Haul Flights (1,500 km to 3,500 km)**:
  - *Delay 3+ hours*: **£350.00 (€400)** per passenger.
  - *Example*: London to Athens, Rome, Madrid, or Canary Islands.
- **Long-Haul Flights (Over 3,500 km)**:
  - *Delay between 3 and 4 hours*: **£260.00 (€300)** per passenger (50% reduced rate).
  - *Delay 4+ hours*: **£520.00 (€600)** per passenger.
  - *Example*: London to New York, Dubai, Singapore, or Los Angeles.

### 2. Airline Operational Faults vs Extraordinary Circumstances
To legally qualify for cash compensation, the delay MUST be within the airline's control:
- **Claimable Airline Faults**: Technical aircraft mechanical defects, routine maintenance delays, airline crew shortages, or cabin staff sickness.
- **Exempt Extraordinary Circumstances (No Cash Claim)**: Severe bad weather (heavy fog, snowstorms, volcanic ash), Air Traffic Control (ATC) slot restrictions, industrial action by airport security/ATC staff, or political instability.

### 3. Statutory 'Right to Care' (Food, Drink & Hotel Accommodation)
Regardless of the cause of the delay (even during extraordinary weather):
- If delayed **2+ hours** (short-haul) or **3+ hours** (medium/long-haul), the airline MUST provide **free food and drink vouchers** and **two free phone calls/emails**.
- If delayed **overnight**, the airline MUST provide **free hotel accommodation** and **free taxi transport** between the airport and hotel.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Flight Claim Warnings

1. **Accepting Airline Travel Vouchers Instead of Cash**: Airlines often email delayed passengers offering £100 airline travel vouchers. You have a legal right to demand statutory CASH paid directly to your bank account (£220, £350, or £520). NEVER accept travel vouchers unless they exceed the statutory cash value.
2. **Using Claims Management Companies (30% Fee Deductions)**: Flight claim companies take 30% to 40% of your payout in commission fees. You can claim 100% of your compensation for FREE by submitting a claim form directly on the airline's official website or escalating to **AviationADR** / **CEDR**.
3. **Measuring Delay at Takeoff Instead of Arrival**: UK261 compensation is measured by the delay upon **arrival at the destination gate when the aircraft doors open**, NOT when the plane takes off from the departure runway.
