Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax charged on most goods and services sold by registered businesses in the UK. If your business turnover exceeds the VAT registration threshold of **£90,000**, registering for VAT is mandatory. This calculator helps business owners, freelancers, and consumers quickly add VAT to a price or extract it from a gross total.

To calculate VAT, you must apply the correct rate. Under **HMRC rules**, there are three main rate bands: standard rate (**20%**), reduced rate (**5%**), and zero rate (**0%**). Use the selector above to toggle rates or enter a custom rate.

## ⚙️ UK VAT Rates and Rules

HMRC classifies goods and services under different rates depending on their category:

- **Standard Rate (20%)**: Applies to the majority of goods and services, including electronics, professional services, vehicles, and adult clothing.
- **Reduced Rate (5%)**: Applies to a limited group of items, such as domestic energy (heating fuel and electricity), children's car seats, mobility aids, and certain renovations.
- **Zero Rate (0%)**: Applied to goods that are still VAT taxable but charged at 0%. This includes most supermarket food, children's clothes, books, newspapers, and public transport.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Incorrectly subtracting 20% to remove VAT**: If you subtract 20% directly from a gross price of £120.00, you get £96.00, which is incorrect. You must divide by **1.20** to extract the correct net price of £100.00.
2. **Confusing Zero-Rated with Exempt Goods**: Zero-rated goods count toward your VAT taxable turnover, whereas exempt goods (such as health services, insurance, and education) do not.
3. **Missing the Registration Threshold**: If your rolling 12-month VAT taxable turnover goes over **£90,000**, you have **30 days** to register with HMRC to avoid penalties.
