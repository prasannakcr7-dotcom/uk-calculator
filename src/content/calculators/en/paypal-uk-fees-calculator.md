When selling goods or services online using PayPal in the UK, PayPal automatically deducts a merchant transaction fee from each incoming payment before depositing net funds into your account balance.

Understanding the exact fee structure enables UK freelancers, small businesses, and e-commerce sellers to price products accurately, calculate allowable tax-deductible expenses, and adjust invoice totals so they receive their target net earnings.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Standard UK Domestic Commercial Rate**: **2.90% + 30p** per domestic payment transaction.
- **PayPal QR Code Rate (Above £10)**: **1.50% + 10p** per transaction.
- **PayPal Micropayments Rate**: **5.00% + 5p** per transaction (beneficial for low-value payments under £5).
- **International Cross-Border Rate**: Standard rate plus **1.29% to 1.99% cross-border fee** (e.g. 3.40% + 30p for European payments), plus currency conversion spreads.
- **Friends & Family Payments**: **0.00% fee** for UK domestic transfers funded by bank/debit balance (banned for commercial sales).
- **Tax Deductibility**: All PayPal merchant processing fees are fully tax-deductible allowable business expenses against UK Income Tax or Corporation Tax.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Using Friends & Family for Commercial Sales**: Requesting customers pay via "Friends & Family" to avoid fees violates PayPal terms of service, voids Buyer/Seller Protection, and can result in account suspension.
- **Overlooking Micropayment Accounts**: Merchants selling low-priced items (£2.00 items) on standard rates lose 35.8p per sale (17.9% fee drag); switching to PayPal Micropayments (5% + 5p) cuts fees to 15p per sale.
- **Forgetting Currency Conversion Fees**: Accepting non-GBP payments incurs an additional 3.0% to 4.0% currency conversion margin added to standard merchant fees.
