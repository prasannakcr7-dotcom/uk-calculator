Laying a block paved driveway or porcelain patio in the UK requires calculating individual **paving blocks or slabs**, crushed **MOT Type 1 sub-base aggregate**, **sharp sand bedding**, and **kiln-dried jointing sand**.

Standard UK block pavers measure **200mm long × 100mm wide × 50mm thick**, requiring exactly **50 blocks per square metre**. Factoring in sub-base excavation depths and a **10% cutting waste buffer** guarantees a long-lasting, stable installation.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Driveway & Paving Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Paving Unit Quantities & Formulas
- **Standard Block Paver (200mm × 100mm)**: **50 blocks per sq m**.
- **600mm × 600mm Paving Slab**: **2.78 slabs per sq m**.
- **600mm × 450mm Porcelain Slab**: **3.70 slabs per sq m**.
- **Gross Order Quantity**: `Total Units = Ceiling[ Surface Area (sq m) × Units per sq m × (1 + Waste % ÷ 100) ]`.

### 2. Sub-Base & Bedding Layer Depth Rules (BS 7533)
Hard landscaping requires a multi-layer foundation to prevent sinkage and ruts under vehicle loads:
- **MOT Type 1 Sub-Base (100mm Compacted Depth)**: Crushed limestone/granite sub-base. Requires **~150 kg per sq m** (100mm depth).
- **Sharp Sand Bedding Layer (50mm Depth)**: Screeded uncompacted sharp sand layer. Requires **~75 kg per sq m** (50mm depth).
- **Kiln-Dried Jointing Sand**: Fine silica sand brushed into block joints after laying. Requires **1 bag (20kg) per 15 sq m**.

### 3. Statutory SuDS Drainage Regulations (Front Gardens Over 5 sq m)
Under UK Town and Country Planning regulations:
- Paving a front garden over **5 square metres** with non-permeable materials requires **planning permission** UNLESS the surface drains to a porous border, lawn, or dedicated soakaway under **SuDS (Sustainable Drainage Systems)** guidelines.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Paving Warnings

1. **Inadequate Sub-Base Compaction**: Failing to compact MOT Type 1 in 50mm layers using a heavy vibrating plate compactor (wacker plate) causes car tyres to sink and create dips in the block paving within 12 months.
2. **Brushing Kiln-Dried Sand into Wet Blocks**: Kiln-dried jointing sand MUST be brushed into block joints when both blocks and sand are 100% dry. Applying sand to damp blocks causes sand to stick to block faces.
3. **Placing Paving Above Damp Proof Course (DPC)**: Finished paving levels must sit at least **150mm (2 brick courses) below the house Damp Proof Course (DPC)** to prevent rain splashback from causing rising damp.
