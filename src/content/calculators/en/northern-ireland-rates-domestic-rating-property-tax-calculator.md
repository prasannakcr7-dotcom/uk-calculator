Unlike Great Britain (England, Scotland, and Wales) which uses Council Tax bands A to H, **Northern Ireland** operates a unique domestic property tax system known as **Domestic Rates**.

Domestic Rates in Northern Ireland are collected centrally by **Land & Property Services (LPS)**. Rates are calculated as a percentage of your individual property's **assessed capital market value** (as evaluated by LPS), combining two distinct rate multipliers: the **Regional Rate** (set by the Northern Ireland Executive) and the **District Rate** (set by your local council district).

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Domestic Rates Calculation Formula**:
  - `Gross Domestic Rates (£) = LPS Capital Valuation (£) × Combined Rate Multiplier`.
  - `Combined Rate Multiplier = Regional Rate + Local District Council Rate` (typically between **0.89% and 1.02%** of capital value).
- **Maximum Valuation Cap (£400,000)**: Under Northern Ireland legislation, domestic rate calculations are capped at a maximum capital valuation of **£400,000**. Properties valued above £400,000 pay rates based on £400,000.
- **4% Early Payment Discount**: If you pay your annual domestic rates bill in full by the early May deadline (typically 8 May), LPS awards an automatic **4% discount**.
- **10-Month Direct Debit**: Rates can be paid in 10 monthly direct debit instalments from April to January.

  <strong>Example 1: Residential Property in Belfast City Council Valued at £175,000 (4% Early Payment Discount Applied)</strong>

    <li>Council District: Belfast City Council (Combined Multiplier ~0.94%)</li>

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Confusing NI Domestic Rates with Council Tax Bands**: Northern Ireland has no Council Tax bands; rates scale continuously based on exact LPS capital valuation up to £400,000.
- **Missing the 4% Early Payment Deadline**: Missing the early May payment deadline forfeits the 4% discount, requiring full rate payment.
- **Forgetting Lone Pensioner Allowance**: Single pensioners aged 70 or older living alone in Northern Ireland qualify for a 20% discount on their domestic rates bill.
