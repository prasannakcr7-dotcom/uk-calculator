Switching from a fossil fuel heating system (gas, oil, or LPG) to a low-carbon **Air Source Heat Pump (ASHP)** or **Ground Source Heat Pump (GSHP)** is supported by substantial UK government incentives.

Under the statutory **Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS)** in England and Wales, homeowners receive a upfront **£7,500 government grant voucher** alongside a **0% VAT rate** on installation materials and labor.

## ⚙️ Statutory Heat Pump Grant & Efficiency Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) Grant Amounts
- **Air Source Heat Pump (ASHP)**: **£7,500.00 upfront grant voucher**.
- **Ground Source Heat Pump (GSHP)**: **£7,500.00 upfront grant voucher**.
- **Voucher Application**: Your MCS-accredited installer applies directly to Ofgem for the voucher, which is deducted upfront from your installation invoice.

### 2. Devolved Schemes (Scotland & Northern Ireland)
- **Scotland (Home Energy Scotland)**: Offers a **£7,500 grant** (£9,000 for rural/island homes) plus an optional **£7,500 interest-free loan**.
- **Northern Ireland**: Low-carbon heating grants offered through local energy efficiency schemes.

### 3. Statutory 0% VAT Rate & Mandatory Eligibility Criteria
- **0% VAT Rate**: All heat pump installation labor and materials carry **0% VAT** through March 2027.
- **Fossil Fuel System Replacement**: The installation MUST replace an existing fossil fuel system (gas, oil, LPG, or direct electric).
- **MCS Accreditation**: Work must be completed by an **Microgeneration Certification Scheme (MCS)** accredited installer.
- **Property insulation**: Properties must have a valid Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) with no outstanding recommendations for uninsulated loft or cavity walls.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Heat Pump Warnings

1. **Undersized Radiators**: Heat pumps deliver warm water at 45°C to 55°C (compared to 65°C for gas boilers). If existing radiators are too small, they will fail to heat rooms adequately without upgrading to larger double-panel (Type 22) radiators or underfloor heating.
2. **Choosing Non-MCS Installers**: Attempting to install a heat pump using a non-MCS registered plumber completely forfeits your £7,500 BUS grant eligibility and 0% VAT status.
3. **Drafty Property Insulation Shortfalls**: Installing a heat pump in an uninsulated stone or solid-brick property without loft or wall insulation causes excessive electricity consumption and high winter heating bills.

    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No. The Boiler Upgrade Scheme is restricted to existing homes replacing fossil fuel boilers. The only exception is self-build properties constructed for personal ownership.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Are Hybrid Heat Pump systems (gas + heat pump) eligible for BUS grants? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No. Hybrid systems that retain a backup gas or oil boiler are strictly excluded from the Boiler Upgrade Scheme. The system must be a 100% low-carbon heat pump installation.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How long does an Air Source Heat Pump last? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Modern heat pumps have an average operating lifespan of **20 to 25 years**, significantly longer than the 10 to 15-year typical lifespan of standard gas boilers.</p>
    </div>
