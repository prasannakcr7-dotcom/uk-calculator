Your UK **State Pension Age** is the earliest statutory age at which you can claim the DWP **New State Pension**.

Over recent decades, UK legislation has equalized the State Pension age for men and women and introduced scheduled increases from **65 to 66**, then to **67**, and eventually to **68**.

## ⚙️ Statutory State Pension Age Schedules for 2026/27

### 1. Age 66 Baseline (Born 1954 to 1960)
- Individuals born between **6 October 1954 and 5 April 1960** reach State Pension age on their **66th birthday**.

### 2. Transition to Age 67 (Born 1961 to 1976)
- Under the Pensions Act 2014, the State Pension age gradually increases to **67** between **6 April 2026 and 6 April 2028**.
- Individuals born between **6 April 1961 and 5 April 1977** reach State Pension age at **67 Years Old**.

### 3. Schedule for Age 68 (Born 1977 Onward)
- Under current legislation, the State Pension age is scheduled to increase to **68** between **2044 and 2046**.
- Individuals born on or after **6 April 1977** currently have a statutory State Pension age of **68 Years Old** (subject to future parliamentary reviews).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & State Pension Age Warnings

1. **Confusing Private Pension Age with State Pension Age**: You can access private and workplace pension drawdown at the **Normal Minimum Pension Age (NMPA)**, which increases from age 55 to **57 in April 2028**. State Pension age is completely separate and currently 66+.
2. **Assuming You Can Draw State Pension Early**: Unlike private pensions or US Social Security, you CANNOT choose to draw your UK State Pension early for a reduced rate. You MUST reach your statutory State Pension age.
3. **Periodic Parliamentary Reviews**: By law, the UK Government conducts an independent review of the State Pension age every 6 years to assess life expectancy data. The planned transition to age 68 may be brought forward or delayed.
