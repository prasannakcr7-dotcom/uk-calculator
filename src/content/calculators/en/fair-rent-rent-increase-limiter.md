Under the **Rent Act 1977**, tenants with **Regulated Tenancies** (tenancies that began before 15 January 1989) enjoy legal protection known as **Fair Rent**.

Under the **Rent Acts (Maximum Fair Rent) Order 1999**, landlords cannot increase a regulated rent beyond a strict statutory ceiling calculated using **Retail Price Index (RPI) inflation plus 5%**.

## ⚙️ Statutory Maximum Fair Rent Rules for 2026/27

### 1. The Maximum Fair Rent Statutory Formula
When a landlord or tenant applies to the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) Rent Officer to re-register a fair rent:
- **Maximum Cap Formula**: `New Fair Rent Cap = Existing Registered Rent × [ 1 + RPI Change + 0.05 ]`.
- **First Re-Registration Buffer**: For a first re-registration after 1999, the statutory buffer is **7.5%** instead of 5.0%.
- **Subsequent Re-Registrations**: The statutory buffer is **5.0%**.

### 2. The 2-Year Registration Interval
- Once a Fair Rent is formally registered by a VOA Rent Officer, it remains **fixed for 2 years**.
- Neither the landlord nor tenant can apply for a new rent registration until 1 year and 9 months have passed, unless there has been a major physical change to the property (such as major structural improvement).

### 3. Exemption for Major Capital Improvements
- If the landlord carries out substantial physical improvements to the property (e.g. installing central heating, double glazing, or a new extension) that significantly increase its rental market value, the Maximum Fair Rent Cap may be waived by the Rent Officer.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Fair Rent Warnings

1. **Attempting Private Agreements Outside the Rent Register**: Any agreement between a regulated landlord and tenant to pay a rent higher than the registered Fair Rent is legally void. The tenant can recover all overpaid rent through the courts.
2. **Ignoring the VOA Public Register**: Anyone can check existing registered fair rents free of charge on the official **VOA Register of Fair Rents** online portal.
3. **Assuming Fair Rent Applies to Assured Shorthold Tenancies (ASTs)**: Fair Rent rules apply strictly to pre-1989 regulated tenancies. Standard private tenancies created after 15 January 1989 are Assured Shorthold Tenancies (ASTs) governed by standard market rent rules.
