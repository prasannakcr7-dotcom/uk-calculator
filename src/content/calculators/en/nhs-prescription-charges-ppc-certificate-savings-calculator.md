In England, standard NHS prescription charges cost **£9.90 per prescribed item**. For patients requiring regular monthly medication (such as asthma inhalers, blood pressure tablets, or HRT), buying individual prescriptions quickly accumulates into hundreds of pounds per year.

Purchasing an official **NHS Prescription Prepayment Certificate (PPC)** acts like a season ticket for prescriptions. For **£114.50 per year** (payable via 10 Direct Debit installments of £11.45), a 12-Month PPC covers **unlimited NHS prescription items**, saving regular patients over **£240+ per year**.

## ⚙️ Statutory NHS Prescription Rules & Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Single Item Charges vs PPC Certificate Options
- **Single NHS Prescription Item Fee (England)**: **£9.90 per item**.
- **3-Month PPC Certificate**: **£31.25** (covers unlimited items for 3 months; cheaper if you need **4 or more items in 3 months**).
- **12-Month PPC Certificate**: **£114.50** (covers unlimited items for 12 months; cheaper if you need **12 or more items in 12 months**—i.e. just 1 item per month!).
- **HRT (Hormone Replacement Therapy) PPC**: Dedicated 12-Month HRT PPC costs **£19.80**, covering unlimited eligible HRT medicines for 12 months.

### 2. The PPC Financial Savings Formula
- **Pay-per-Item Annual Cost (£)**: `Pay-per-Item = Monthly Prescription Items × 12 × £9.90`.
- **Annual PPC Net Savings (£)**: `Annual Savings = Pay-per-Item Cost – £114.50 (12-Month PPC Cost)`.
- *Example*: A patient taking **3 prescription items per month (36 items per year)**:
  - `Pay-per-Item Cost = 36 × £9.90 = £356.40 per year`.
  - `12-Month PPC Cost = £114.50 per year`.
  - `Annual Financial Savings = £356.40 – £114.50 = £241.90 Saved per Year`.

### 3. Devolved Nations: 100% Free Prescriptions
- **Scotland**: NHS prescriptions are **100% FREE for all residents** (abolished in 2011).
- **Wales**: NHS prescriptions are **100% FREE for all residents** (abolished in 2007).
- **Northern Ireland**: NHS prescriptions are **100% FREE for all residents** (abolished in 2010).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Prescription Warnings

1. **Paying per Item When Needing 2+ Prescriptions a Month**: Anyone in England taking 2 or more prescription items a month should NEVER pay per item. A 12-Month PPC pays for itself after just 12 items.
2. **Forgetting Medical Exemption Certificates (MedEx)**: Patients with specified medical conditions (such as diabetes treated with insulin/medication, epilepsy, hypothyroidism, or a permanent stoma) qualify for a **100% Free Medical Exemption Certificate (MedEx)** issued by their GP.
3. **Overpaying for Combined HRT + Non-HRT Prescriptions**: The £19.80 HRT PPC covers *only* eligible HRT medicines. If you take non-HRT medications as well, buy the standard 12-Month PPC (£114.50) which covers *all* NHS prescriptions.
