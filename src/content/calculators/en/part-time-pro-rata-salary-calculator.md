A pro-rata salary is a proportional salary paid to part-time workers based on the Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) salary advertised for the role.

Under UK employment law, part-time employees have the statutory right not to be treated less favorably than comparable full-time workers.

## ⚙️ Pro-Rata Salary Formulas & Equal Treatment Rules

### 1. Pro-Rata Salary Calculation Formula
Pro-rata calculations compare part-time working hours against the standard full-time week (typically 37.5 hours):
- **FTE Ratio**: `Part-Time Hours / Full-Time Hours (37.5)`
- **Pro-Rata Annual Salary**: `Full-Time Equivalent Salary × FTE Ratio`
- **Pro-Rata Monthly Salary**: `Pro-Rata Annual Salary / 12`

### 2. Statutory Legal Equal Treatment Protection
Under the **Part-Time Workers (Prevention of Less Favourable Treatment) Regulations 2000**, part-time employees must receive:
- The **same hourly pay rate** as full-time workers in the same role.
- Pro-rata statutory holiday entitlement (5.6 weeks pro-rata).
- Equal pro-rata access to company pension schemes, bonuses, and training opportunities.

## 📊 Practical Pro-Rata Worked Examples

Below are two worked calculation examples illustrating pro-rata salary ratios:

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 1: Working 22.5 hours/week on a £36,000 FTE salary (37.5h full-time)</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Full-Time Equivalent Salary (FTE): **£36,000.00 / year**</li>
    <li>Part-Time Working Hours: **22.5 Hours / week**</li>
    <li>FTE Ratio: 22.5 ÷ 37.5 = **0.60 (60.0% FTE)**</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Calculation: £36,000.00 × 60.0% = £21,600.00 gross annual salary.</p>
  <div class="net-highlight">Pro-Rata Gross Annual Salary: **£21,600.00 / year** (£1,800.00 per month)</div>
</div>

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 2: Working 18.75 hours/week (3 days) on a £40,000 FTE salary</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Full-Time Equivalent Salary (FTE): **£40,000.00 / year**</li>
    <li>Part-Time Working Hours: **18.75 Hours / week**</li>
    <li>FTE Ratio: 18.75 ÷ 37.5 = **0.50 (50.0% FTE)**</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Calculation: £40,000.00 × 50.0% = £20,000.00 gross annual salary.</p>
  <div class="net-highlight">Pro-Rata Gross Annual Salary: **£20,000.00 / year** (£1,666.67 per month)</div>
</div>

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Job Offer Warnings

1. **Confusing Advertised FTE Salary with Actual Pay**: Job adverts frequently state "Salary: £35,000 pro-rata." If you work 3 days a week (60% FTE), your actual gross salary will be **£21,000**, not £35,000.
2. **Forgetting Tax Threshold Advantages**: Because Income Tax and NI are progressive, a 60% pro-rata salary often yields **more than 60% of full-time net take-home pay** due to the £12,570 Personal Allowance.
3. **Miscalculating Term-Time Only Contracts**: Term-time workers (e.g. school teaching assistants) have their annual salary calculated pro-rata for both weekly hours AND weeks worked per year (e.g. 39 term weeks instead of 52).
