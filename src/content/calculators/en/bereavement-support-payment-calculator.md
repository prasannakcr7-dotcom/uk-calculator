**Bereavement Support Payment (BSP)** is a financial welfare benefit paid by the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) to help individuals in the UK adjust financially following the death of a spouse, civil partner, or cohabitating partner.

Understanding the difference between the **Higher Rate (£3,500 lump sum + £350/month for parents)** and the **Standard Rate (£2,500 lump sum + £100/month)** ensures bereaved families receive their full statutory entitlement.

## ⚙️ Statutory Bereavement Support Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Higher Rate (Households with Dependent Children)
If you were pregnant or entitled to Child Benefit when your partner died:
- **Initial One-Off Lump Sum**: **£3,500.00** (paid immediately upon claim approval).
- **Monthly Instalments**: **£350.00 per month** paid for **18 months**.
- **Total Award Value**: **£9,800.00 total statutory support**.

### 2. Standard Rate (Households without Dependent Children)
If you were under State Pension age and have no dependent children:
- **Initial One-Off Lump Sum**: **£2,500.00** (paid immediately upon claim approval).
- **Monthly Instalments**: **£100.00 per month** paid for **18 months**.
- **Total Award Value**: **£4,300.00 total statutory support**.

### 3. Tax & Means-Testing Status
- Bereavement Support Payment is **100% tax-free**.
- It is **NOT means-tested** against savings, property, or employment income.
- It is **completely exempt from the UK Benefit Cap** and is ignored as income for Universal Credit for the first 12 months.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Bereavement Warnings

1. **Claiming After 3 Months Limits Lump Sum**: To receive the full lump sum payment, you MUST submit your claim within **3 months of your partner's death**. Claiming up to 21 months after death is allowed, but payments will be backdated pro-rata and the lump sum may be forfeited.
2. **State Pension Age Limit**: You must have been under State Pension age when your partner died. If you were already receiving the State Pension, you cannot claim Bereavement Support Payment.
3. **National Insurance Requirement**: Your deceased partner must have paid Class 1 or Class 2 National Insurance contributions in at least 1 tax year, or died due to an industrial disease/accident.
