Predicting your child's future adult height is a fascinating blend of genetics and growth environment. Pediatricians use the clinically validated **Mid-Parental Height Method (Tanner's Formula)** to project adult stature based on parental genetics.

Genetics accounts for approximately **80% of final adult height**, with environmental factors such as nutrition, sleep, physical activity, and overall health accounting for the remaining 20%.

## ⚙️ Tanner's Mid-Parental Height Formula Math

Pediatric clinical guidelines calculate target adult height using biological parental heights:

### 1. Formula for Boys
- **Formula**: `(Mother's Height + Father's Height + 13 cm) / 2`
- **Concept**: The +13 cm offset accounts for the average height difference between adult men and women.

### 2. Formula for Girls
- **Formula**: `(Mother's Height + Father's Height - 13 cm) / 2`
- **Concept**: The -13 cm offset adjusts for female adult growth trajectories.

### 3. Clinical Target Range (95% Confidence Interval)
Genetics provides a target range rather than a single exact number. Most healthy children reach a final adult height within **±6.5 cm (2.5 inches)** of their calculated Mid-Parental Height.

## 📊 Practical Child Height Worked Examples

Below are two worked clinical calculation examples for boys and girls:

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 1: Predicting height for a Boy (Mother: 165 cm, Father: 180 cm)</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Mother's height: **165 cm**</li>
    <li>Father's height: **180 cm**</li>
    <li>Formula adjustment for boys: **+13 cm**</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Calculation: (165 + 180 + 13) ÷ 2 = 358 ÷ 2 = 179 cm.</p>
  <div class="net-highlight">Predicted Adult Height: **179.0 cm (5 ft 10 in)** [Range: 172.5 cm to 185.5 cm]</div>
</div>

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 2: Predicting height for a Girl (Mother: 165 cm, Father: 180 cm)</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Mother's height: **165 cm**</li>
    <li>Father's height: **180 cm**</li>
    <li>Formula adjustment for girls: **-13 cm**</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Calculation: (165 + 180 - 13) ÷ 2 = 332 ÷ 2 = 166 cm.</p>
  <div class="net-highlight">Predicted Adult Height: **166.0 cm (5 ft 5 in)** [Range: 159.5 cm to 172.5 cm]</div>
</div>

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Pediatric Warnings

1. **Doubling Height at Age 2 Shortcut**: While the popular rule of "double their height at age 2" gives a quick estimate, it is less clinically accurate than Tanner's Mid-Parental formula because toddler growth spurts vary.
2. **Ignoring Growth Spurt Timing**: Constitutional growth delays (being a "late bloomer") can cause a teenager to temporarily fall behind peers before experiencing a rapid growth spurt between ages 14 and 17.
3. **Medical Factors Affecting Growth**: Severe chronic illnesses, human growth hormone (HGH) deficiencies, or malnutrition can alter genetic potential. Consult your GP if your child crosses two or more centile lines on their NHS Red Book growth chart.
