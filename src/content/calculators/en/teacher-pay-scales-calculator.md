Qualified teacher salaries in state-maintained schools in England and Wales are governed by the statutory **School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document (STPCD)**.

Understanding teacher pay scale points (Main Pay Range M1–M6, Upper Pay Range U1–U3), regional London allowances, Teaching and Learning Responsibility (TLR) payments, and **Teachers' Pension Scheme** deductions ensures transparent salary calculation.

## ⚙️ Statutory Teacher Pay Ranges & London Allowances for 2026/27

### 1. Main Pay Range (M1 to M6) Benchmarks (Rest of England)
- **M1 (NQT / ECT Entry)**: **£31,650 per year**.
- **M2**: £33,800 per year.
- **M3**: £36,150 per year.
- **M4**: £38,650 per year.
- **M5**: £41,500 per year.
- **M6 (Top of Main Range)**: **£44,870 per year**.

### 2. Regional London Weighting Scale Spans
- **Inner London**: M1 starts at **£36,745**; M6 reaches **£49,084**; U3 reaches **£56,959**.
- **Outer London**: M1 starts at **£34,514**; M6 reaches **£46,853**; U3 reaches **£51,179**.
- **Fringe**: M1 starts at **£32,950**; M6 reaches **£46,150**.

### 3. Tiered Teachers' Pension Member Contributions
Teachers' Pension Scheme member contributions are deducted from gross pay:
- **Up to £34,289**: 7.4%
- **£34,290 to £46,158**: 8.6%
- **£46,159 to £54,729**: 9.6%
- **£54,730 to £72,589**: 10.2%

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Teacher Payroll Warnings

1. **Confusing Main Pay Progression with Upper Pay Threshold (Post Threshold)**: Moving from M6 to U1 (Upper Pay Range) is NOT automatic. Teachers must apply for Post-Threshold Standards assessment to demonstrate substantial and sustained competence.
2. **TLR Payment Tier Categories**: Teaching and Learning Responsibility payments are divided into three bands: TLR 1 (£9,272 to £15,690), TLR 2 (£3,214 to £7,847), and TLR 3 fixed-term projects (£639 to £3,169).
3. **Teachers' Pension Tax Relief Automatic Adjustment**: Teachers' Pension contributions are deducted **before Income Tax** under net pay arrangements, providing immediate 20% or 40% tax relief on pay slips.
