If you have suffered a **personal injury** in the UK through no fault of your own (e.g. a road traffic accident, accident at work, slip/trip in a public place, or medical negligence), you are legally entitled to claim financial **compensation**.

UK personal injury compensation consists of two main elements: **General Damages** (compensation for physical pain, mental suffering, and loss of amenity calculated using the official **Judicial College Guidelines**) and **Special Damages** (reimbursement for actual financial out-of-pocket losses like lost earnings and private medical care).

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Personal Injury Rules & Guidelines for 2026/27

### 1. General Damages vs Special Damages
- **General Damages (Pain & Suffering)**:
  - Calculated using the official **Judicial College Guidelines (17th Edition)**.
  - *Minor Soft Tissue / Neck Injury*: **£1,500 to £3,500**.
  - *Moderate Leg / Arm Fracture*: **£12,000 to £25,000**.
  - *Severe Surgical / Joint Injury*: **£35,000 to £60,000**.
  - *Catastrophic Life-Changing Injury*: **£120,000 to £350,000+**.
- **Special Damages (Financial Out-of-Pocket Losses)**:
  - Reimburses 100% of past and future financial losses resulting from the injury (e.g. net lost wages, private physiotherapy fees, prescription costs, travel expenses, and family care hours).

### 2. LASPO Act 2012 & The 25% Success Fee Cap
Under the **Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (LASPO)**:
- Personal injury claims operate on a **No Win No Fee (Conditional Fee Agreement - CFA)** basis.
- If your claim wins, your solicitor is legally entitled to charge a **Success Fee**, but by law this fee is capped at a **maximum of 25% of your damages award** (excluding future financial loss awards).
- *Example*: A client awarded **£15,000 General Damages + £5,000 Special Damages (£20,000 total)** with a **25% Success Fee cap**:
  - `Gross Compensation Award = £20,000.00`.
  - `Solicitor 25% Success Fee = £20,000 × 25% = £5,000.00`.
  - `Net Payout Received by Client = £20,000 – £5,000 = £15,000.00 net`.

### 3. Statutory 3-Year Limitation Period
- Under the **Limitation Act 1980**, personal injury claims MUST be formally issued in court within **3 years of the date of the accident** (or the date of knowledge of the injury).
- *Exceptions*: Children (3-year clock starts on their 18th birthday, expiring on their 21st birthday) and individuals lacking mental capacity (no time limit).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Injury Claim Warnings

1. **Accepting an Early 'Lowball' Settlement Offer from Insurers**: Insurance companies frequently approach injured victims directly after an accident offering a fast £1,500 settlement before a medical report is completed. NEVER accept an early offer without an independent medico-legal report.
2. **Failing to Keep Receipts for Special Damages**: You cannot claim Special Damages without empirical proof. Always keep payslips proving lost wages, travel receipts, and invoices for private treatment.
3. **Whiplash Tariff Limits (Civil Liability Act 2018)**: Minor RTA whiplash injuries lasting under 2 years are subject to fixed government whiplash tariffs (£240 to £4,345) and must be submitted via the Official Injury Claim (OIC) online portal without recovering legal costs.

    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No. Personal injury compensation awards (both General and Special Damages) are 100% EXEMPT from UK Income Tax and Capital Gains Tax.</p>
