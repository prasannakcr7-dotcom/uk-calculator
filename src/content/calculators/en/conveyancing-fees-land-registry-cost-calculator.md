**Conveyancing** is the legal process of transferring home ownership from a seller to a buyer in the UK.

Whether buying a freehold house or a leasehold flat, calculating solicitor **legal fees**, mandatory **third-party disbursements (local authority searches)**, and official **HM Land Registry registration scale fees** ensures complete cost transparency before exchanging contracts.

## ⚙️ Statutory HM Land Registry & Conveyancing Rules for 2026/27

### 1. HM Land Registry Scale 1 Registration Fees (Digital Applications)
When purchasing a property, HM Land Registry charges a statutory registration fee based on purchase price (Scale 1 digital rates):
- **Up to £100,000**: **£45.00**.
- **£100,001 to £200,000**: **£95.00**.
- **£200,001 to £500,000**: **£150.00**.
- **£500,001 to £1,000,000**: **£295.00**.
- **Over £1,000,000**: **£500.00**.
- *Paper Applications*: Submitting via postal paper forms doubles the Land Registry fee (e.g. £150 increases to £300).

### 2. Legal Disbursements (Third-Party Search Fees)
Disbursements are mandatory third-party fees collected by your conveyancer:
- **Local Authority Search**: **£150 to £250** (Checks planning history, proposed road schemes, and conservation zones).
- **Water & Drainage Search**: **£50 to £100** (Verifies connection to public sewers and mains water).
- **Environmental Risk Search**: **£50 to £80** (Checks contaminated land, flood risk, and ground subsidence).
- **CHAPS Bank Telegraphic Transfer Fee**: **£25 to £45** per wire transfer.

### 3. Leasehold vs Freehold Legal Fee Differences
- **Leasehold Supplement**: Buying a leasehold flat requires reviewing complex leases, service charge accounts, and obtaining a **Leasehold Management Pack (LPE1 form)** from the freeholder, adding **£300 to £500** in extra legal fees.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Conveyancing Warnings

1. **Unquoted 'Hidden' Legal Extras**: Always request a fixed-fee quote complying with SRA Transparency Rules. Beware of conveyancers who exclude mandatory fees (such as mortgage handling, SDLT filing, or bankruptcy search fees) from initial quotes.
2. **Conveyancing Delays on Property Chains**: The average UK property conveyancing transaction takes **12 to 16 weeks** from offer acceptance to completion day. Responding to solicitor enquiries within 48 hours keeps chain progress moving.
3. **Impersonation & Wire Fraud Warnings**: Fraudulent cybercriminals intercept conveyancing emails to send fake bank details for deposit wire transfers. Always verify conveyancer bank details over the telephone before transferring funds.
