In the UK welfare system, individuals facing long-term illness, physical or mental health conditions, or disabilities can claim **New Style Employment and Support Allowance (ESA)** and **Personal Independence Payment (PIP)**.

Understanding how **ESA Support Group rates (£138.20/wk)** and **PIP Daily Living / Mobility components** work together ensures disabled individuals receive full statutory financial support.

## ⚙️ Statutory ESA & PIP Benefit Rates for 2026/27

### 1. New Style Employment & Support Allowance (ESA)
New Style ESA is paid if an illness or disability limits your ability to work and you paid Class 1/2 National Insurance:
- **Assessment Phase (First 13 Weeks)**: **£90.50 per week**.
- **Work-Related Activity Group**: **£90.50 per week**.
- **Support Group (Severe Limited Capability)**: **£138.20 per week** (£276.40 every 2 weeks).

### 2. Personal Independence Payment (PIP)
PIP is a 100% tax-free benefit helping with extra living or mobility costs. PIP is **NOT means-tested** against savings or income:
- **Daily Living Component**:
  - Standard Rate: **£72.65 per week**.
  - Enhanced Rate: **£108.55 per week**.
- **Mobility Component**:
  - Standard Rate: **£28.70 per week**.
  - Enhanced Rate: **£75.75 per week**.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Disability Benefit Warnings

1. **Confusing PIP with Means-Tested Benefits**: PIP is NOT means-tested. You can receive the maximum PIP award regardless of whether you earn £100,000 salary or have £100,000 in savings.
2. **Work Capability Assessment (WCA) Timelines**: When applying for ESA, you must submit Fit Notes from your GP while completing the WCA questionnaire (Form ESA50) and attending a DWP medical assessment.
3. **Motability Scheme Vehicle Exchange**: Claimants receiving the Enhanced Rate Mobility component of PIP (£75.75/wk) can choose to exchange their mobility payment to lease a brand-new car, wheelchair-accessible vehicle, or scooter through the official **Motability Scheme**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<div class="faq-sec">
  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Can I work while receiving New Style ESA? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Yes. You can undertake 'Permitted Work' while receiving ESA, provided you work **under 16 hours per week** and earn **under £183.50 per week**. Permitted Work must be reported to DWP on Form PW1.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Is Adult Disability Payment (ADP) different in Scotland? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Yes. In Scotland, Personal Independence Payment (PIP) has been replaced by **Adult Disability Payment (ADP)** administered by Social Security Scotland. Payment rates and component tiers remain identical to PIP.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Are PIP and ESA payments taxable under UK Income Tax? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>PIP is 100% tax-free. Contribution-based New Style ESA is taxable income, though no National Insurance is deducted. Income-related legacy ESA was non-taxable.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How does receiving PIP increase other benefit entitlements? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Receiving PIP acts as a gateway benefit. Qualifying for PIP entitles your carer to claim **Carer's Allowance (£81.90/wk)**, exempts your household from the Benefit Cap, and adds Disability Premiums to housing support.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
