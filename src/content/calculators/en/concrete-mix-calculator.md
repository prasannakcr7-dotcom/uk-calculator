Pouring a **concrete slab**, shed base, patio foundation, or footings in the UK requires calculating the total wet **concrete volume in cubic metres (m³)** and raw material quantities.

Understanding standard **mix proportions (such as 1:2:4 C20 concrete)** allows DIYers and builders to estimate exact **25kg cement bags**, **jumbo bulk bags of aggregate/ballast (850kg)**, or order volumetric **ready-mix concrete trucks**.

## ⚙️ Statutory Concrete Mix Rules & Strength Classes for 2026/27

### 1. Concrete Volume & Density Formulas
- **Wet Concrete Volume**: `Volume (m³) = Length (m) × Width (m) × Depth (m)`.
- **Wet Concrete Density**: Standard wet concrete weighs approximately **2,400 kg per cubic metre (2.4 tonnes/m³)**.
- *Example*: A garden shed base slab measuring **4.0m long × 3.0m wide × 100mm (0.10m) deep**:
  - `Volume = 4.0m × 3.0m × 0.10m = 1.20 m³`.
  - `Total Wet Weight = 1.20 m³ × 2,400 kg/m³ = 2,880 kg`.

### 2. Standard UK Concrete Mix Proportions (By Volume)
- **1 : 2 : 4 General Structural / Slabs (C20 / GEN 3)**:
  - 1 Part Cement : 2 Parts Sharp Sand : 4 Parts 20mm Coarse Aggregate (or 1 part Cement to 6 parts Combined All-In Ballast).
  - *Yield for 1.20 m³*: Requires **14 bags of 25kg Cement (350kg)** + **2.8 Bulk Bags of Ballast (2,530kg)**.
- **1 : 1.5 : 3 Heavy-Duty Driveways (C30 / PAV 1)**: Higher cement ratio for freeze-thaw and heavy vehicle resistance.
- **1 : 3 : 6 Mass Foundations / Trench Fill (C15 / GEN 1)**: Lower strength mix suitable for unreinforced foundations and sub-bases.

### 3. Mixing Method Choice: Hand Mix vs Ready-Mix Truck
- **Hand / Site Mixer**: Economical for volumes under **1.5 m³** (~18 cement bags).
- **Ready-Mix Volumetric Truck**: Essential for volumes over **1.5 m³ to 2.0 m³**. Mini-mix trucks deliver up to 4.0 m³ of fresh concrete mixed directly on-site to exact volume.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Concrete Warnings

1. **Undersizing Slab Thickness for Vehicles**: Shed bases require a minimum **100mm (4 inches)** slab depth over a 75mm compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base. Driveways accommodating cars require a minimum **150mm (6 inches)** depth with steel mesh reinforcement (A142 mesh).
2. **Adding Too Much Water (Lowering Strength)**: Excess water makes concrete easier to pour, but severely reduces final compressive strength and causes surface cracking and dusting.
3. **Failing to Compact and Cure Properly**: Fresh concrete must be compacted using a tamping board or poker vibrator to remove trapped air pockets, then covered with polythene sheeting to cure for at least **7 days**.
