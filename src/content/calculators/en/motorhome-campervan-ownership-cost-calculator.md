Owning a **motorhome or campervan** (e.g. VW California, Auto-Trail, or Swift) offers ultimate freedom for UK staycations and European road trips.

However, calculating the true **annual cost of motorhome ownership** requires factoring in **diesel fuel consumption (25 to 30 MPG)**, **DVLA TC11 road tax (£345/yr)**, **specialist insurance**, **habitation damp checks**, **campsite pitch fees**, and **winter storage**.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Motorhome Tax, License & Safety Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Statutory DVLA Motorhome VED Tax Classes
- **Private Heavy Goods / Motorhome Class (TC11 / TC10)**:
  - *Motorhomes registered under 3,500kg MAM*: Standard VED rate **£345.00 per year**.
  - *Motorhomes over 3,500kg MAM (TC11)*: **£165.00 per year** (Private HGV tax class).

### 2. Driving License Weight Limits (Category B vs C1)
- **Category B License (Passed Test After 1 Jan 1997)**: Permits driving motorhomes with a Maximum Authorised Mass (MAM) up to **3,500kg (3.5 tonnes)**.
- **Category C1 License**: Required to drive motorhomes exceeding **3,500kg MAM** up to 7,500kg. Drivers who passed their UK test *before* 1 January 1997 automatically retain C1 grandfather rights.

### 3. AWS Annual Habitation & Damp Inspection
- Motorhome bodies require an annual **Approved Workshop Scheme (AWS) Habitation Inspection (£200 to £300)**.
- Inspections test **LPG gas tightness**, **230V mains and 12V leisure battery electrical systems**, **smoke/CO alarms**, and perform a **36-point damp moisture check** across timber body panels.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Motorhome Warnings

1. **Ignoring Undetected Bodywork Damp Ingress**: Water ingress through roof seams and window seals causes structural timber rot inside sandwich panels. Repairing structural damp costs **£2,000 to £5,000+**. Always demand a fresh damp report before buying a used campervan.
2. **Exceeding Payload Weight Limits**: Overloading a motorhome beyond its plate MAM weight leads to DVSA roadside weighbridge fines (£300) and invalidates insurance. Always calculate net payload (passengers + full water tanks + gas bottles + bicycles).
3. **Invalidating Insurance via Driveway Storage**: If your insurance policy specifies storage at a CaSSOA Gold facility to secure a 20% discount, storing the motorhome on your home driveway invalidates theft claims.
