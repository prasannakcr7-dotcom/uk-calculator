The **Teachers' Pension Scheme (TPS)** is a statutory public sector defined benefit pension scheme provided to state school teachers, academy staff, further education tutors, and university lecturers across England and Wales.

Under modern TPS rules (the 2015 Career Average scheme), teachers accrue guaranteed index-linked retirement income based on a **Career Average Revalued Earnings (CARE)** structure with an accrual rate of **1/57th (1.754%)** of pensionable earnings earned each year.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **CARE Pension Accrual Formula**:
  - `Annual Guaranteed Pension Accrued = Pensionable Salary × (1 / 57)`.
  - Accrued pension is revalued annually in active service at **CPI inflation + 1.6%**.
- **Tiered Member Contribution Rates (2026/27)**:
  - **Up to £32,135**: **7.4%** member contribution rate.
  - **£32,136 to £43,259**: **8.6%** member contribution rate.
  - **£43,260 to £51,292**: **9.6%** member contribution rate.
  - **£51,293 to £67,979**: **10.2%** member contribution rate.
  - **£67,980 to £92,697**: **11.3%** member contribution rate.
  - **Over £92,697**: **11.7%** member contribution rate.
- **Employer Contribution Rate**: Schools and local authorities contribute a massive **28.68%** of each teacher's gross salary into the scheme.
- **Normal Pension Age (NPA)**: Equal to your UK **State Pension Age** (currently 66, rising to 67).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Taking Early Retirement Before Normal Pension Age (NPA)**: Drawing your TPS pension before State Pension Age incurs permanent actuarial reductions (approx. 4% to 5% per year taken early).
- **Ignoring Tax Relief on Member Contributions**: Pension contributions are deducted before PAYE Income Tax is calculated, reducing your effective out-of-pocket cost.
- **McCloud Remedy Transferred Service**: Teachers with service prior to April 2022 benefit from the McCloud remedy, allowing a choice between final salary (1/60th or 1/80th) and CARE benefits for 2015-2022 service.
