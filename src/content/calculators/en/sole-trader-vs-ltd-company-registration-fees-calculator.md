When launching a new business venture in the UK, choosing between operating as a **Sole Trader** or incorporating a **Limited Company (Ltd)** involves contrasting administrative obligations, privacy considerations, and statutory registration fees.

Registering as a Sole Trader is completely **free of charge** through HMRC Self Assessment. Incorporating a UK Limited Company requires registering with **Companies House**, which incurs statutory setup fees, annual Confirmation Statement filing fees, and optional registered address privacy charges.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Sole Trader Setup & Annual Fees**:
  - Initial HMRC Registration: **£0.00 (Free)**
  - Annual Statutory Registry Fee: **£0.00 (Free)**
  - Accounting & Admin: Simple Self Assessment Tax Return.
- **Limited Company Setup & Annual Statutory Fees (2026/27)**:
  - **Digital Incorporation Fee**: **£50.00** (increased under Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act reforms).
  - **Paper Incorporation Fee**: **£71.00**.
  - **Annual Confirmation Statement (CS01)**: **£34.00 per year** (digital) or **£62.00 per year** (paper).
  - **Registered Office Address Service**: **£30.00 to £50.00 per year** (optional service to keep home address off public registers).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Publishing Home Addresses on Companies House**: Using your personal home address as a Limited Company registered office makes it publicly visible on the Companies House online database forever. Use a professional registered office service (£30-£40/yr) before incorporating to preserve privacy.
- **Forgetting Annual Confirmation Statements**: Failing to file your annual Companies House Confirmation Statement (CS01) leads to company dissolution and director prosecution.
- **Assuming Ltd Accountancy Costs are Identical to Sole Trader**: Preparing full micro-entity or small company statutory accounts (FRS 105) for a Ltd company typically costs £500–£1,500/yr in accountancy fees compared to £250–£500/yr for a sole trader return.

    <div class="faq-content">

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Is registering as a Sole Trader completely free in the UK?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Yes. Registering as a Sole Trader with HMRC for Self Assessment and Class 2/4 National Insurance is 100% free. There are no statutory startup registration fees or recurring annual public registry filing charges for sole traders.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is a Companies House Confirmation Statement?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>A Confirmation Statement (Form CS01) is a statutory annual filing that confirms your limited company details (directors, shareholders, registered office, SIC code, and PSC details) are accurate on the public register. It carries an annual digital filing fee of £34.00.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Why should I use a registered office address service?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Companies House registers are publicly accessible to anyone online. If you use your personal home address as your company's registered office, your address will be indexed permanently on search engines and public records. A registered office service provides a professional commercial address for public display.</p>
    </div>
