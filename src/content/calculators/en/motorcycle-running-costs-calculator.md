Riding a **motorcycle or scooter** in the UK is one of the most cost-effective forms of motorized transport, delivering exceptional fuel economy (**75 to 100+ MPG for 125cc commuters**), low road tax (**£25 to £55/year**), and bus lane commuting privileges in major UK cities.

Calculating your total **motorcycle running costs**—combining fuel, DVLA bike VED tax, Class 1/2 MOT fees, servicing, and insurance—reveals the true cost per mile of riding a motor bike.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Motorcycle Tax & Maintenance Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Statutory DVLA Motorcycle VED Road Tax Bands
DVLA levies VED road tax on motorcycles based on engine displacement (cc):
- **Up to 150cc (e.g. 125cc Scooters / Learner Bikes)**: **£25.00 per year**.
- **151cc to 400cc (e.g. 300cc Maxi-Scooters / A2 Bikes)**: **£55.00 per year**.
- **401cc to 600cc (e.g. Middleweight Commuters / Adventure Bikes)**: **£80.00 per year**.
- **Over 600cc (e.g. 1000cc Superbikes / Large Tourers)**: **£111.00 per year**.

### 2. Official Class 1 & 2 Motorcycle MOT Test Fees
- **Statutory Maximum MOT Fee**: The DVSA caps the maximum fee for a motorcycle MOT test at **£29.65** (Class 1 & 2 vehicles) or **£37.80** for a motorcycle with a sidecar.
- **Annual Servicing & Tires**: Motorcycle tires wear faster than car tires (typical rear tire lifespan is 4,000 to 8,000 miles). Basic annual servicing averages **£80 to £180** depending on engine size.

### 3. Motorcycle Fuel Economy Benchmarks
- **125cc Learner / Commuter (e.g. Honda PCX125 / CB125F)**: **75 to 100+ MPG** (10.1p per mile fuel cost).
- **300cc - 500cc A2 Bikes (e.g. Honda CB500X / Yamaha MT-03)**: **60 to 70 MPG** (12.7p per mile fuel cost).
- **600cc - 750cc Middleweights (e.g. Yamaha MT-07 / Kawasaki Z650)**: **50 to 58 MPG** (15.5p per mile fuel cost).
- **1000cc+ Superbikes / Heavy Tourers (e.g. BMW R1250GS / Fireblade)**: **38 to 45 MPG** (19.8p per mile fuel cost).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Motorcycle Warnings

1. **Underestimating Security Lock Requirements**: Motorcycle theft is a major risk in UK cities. Failing to use a **Thatcham-approved Ground Anchor & Disc Lock** can invalidate insurance policies or result in high £1,000+ insurance excess fees if stolen.
2. **Ignoring Winter Chain and Sprocket Maintenance**: Riding on salted UK roads in winter rapidly corrodes motorcycle drive chains. Cleaning and lubricating your chain every 300 miles prevents premature £150 chain and sprocket replacement.
3. **Riding Beyond CBT License Expiry**: A Compulsory Basic Training (CBT) certificate for 125cc motorcycles is valid for **2 years**. Riding after your 2-year CBT expires invalidates your provisional license and insurance.

    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Do motorcycles pay the London Congestion Charge or ULEZ? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Motorcycles are 100% EXEMPT from the London Congestion Charge. Almost all motorcycles manufactured after July 2007 (Euro 3 standards) are also EXEMPT from the £12.50 daily ULEZ charge.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>In London (TfL red routes) and many UK cities (such as Birmingham, Bristol, and Belfast), motorcycles are legally permitted to use designated bus lanes to bypass commuter traffic.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

    <button class="faq-trigger">What clothing gear is legally compulsory for motorcycle riders in the UK? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Under UK law, the ONLY compulsory safety item is an approved protective safety helmet (ECE 22.05 or ECE 22.06 standard). However, CE-approved armored jackets, gloves, trousers, and boots are strongly recommended.</p>
