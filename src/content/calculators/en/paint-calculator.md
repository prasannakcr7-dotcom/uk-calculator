Calculating how much **paint in litres** you need for interior walls, ceilings, skirting boards, or exterior masonry is a crucial step in home redecoration projects.

Standard UK emulsion paints cover approximately **10 to 14 square metres per litre per coat**. Factoring in **wall surface area**, **number of coats (typically 2 coats)**, and a **10% roller/tray spillage buffer** allows you to buy the exact tin combination (**1L, 2.5L, 5L, or 10L trade buckets**) without wasting money on unused paint.

## ⚙️ Industry Paint Spreading Rates & Rules for 2026/27

### 1. The Paint Volume Formula
- **Cumulative Area**: `Total Coated Area (sq m) = Wall Surface Area (sq m) × Number of Coats`.
- **Exact Volume Required**: `Litres = (Total Coated Area ÷ Coverage Rate) × 1.10` (includes 10% spillage and tray buffer).
- *Example*: Painting **35 sq m of walls with 2 coats of emulsion (12 sq m/L coverage rate)**:
  - `Total Coated Area = 35 × 2 = 70 sq m`.
  - `Litres = (70 ÷ 12) × 1.10 = 6.42 Litres`.

### 2. Standard Paint Spreading Rates (sq m per Litre)
Coverage varies depending on paint formula and wall porosity:
- **Standard Matt / Vinyl Silk Emulsion**: **12 to 14 sq m per Litre**.
- **Wood Satinwood / Eggshell / Gloss**: **10 to 12 sq m per Litre**.
- **Exterior Masonry Paint**: **6 to 8 sq m per Litre** (due to rough brickwork/render absorption).
- **Mist Coat on Fresh Unprimed Plaster**: **6 sq m per Litre** (high suction).

### 3. UK Retail & Trade Tin Sizes
UK paints are sold in standardized container capacities:
- **1.0 Litre Tester / Trim Tin**: Ideal for small touch-ups or feature trims.
- **2.5 Litre Standard Can**: Suitable for small bedrooms or bathrooms (~30 sq m 1-coat coverage).
- **5.0 Litre Large Can**: The most popular size for average living rooms and double bedrooms (~60 sq m 1-coat coverage).
- **10.0 Litre Trade Bucket**: Economical trade size for whole-house redecoration or ceilings.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Paint Warnings

1. **Painting Over Fresh Dry Plaster Without a Mist Coat**: Applying full-strength emulsion directly onto new dry plaster causes the plaster to suck moisture out of the paint, leading to cracking and peeling. Always apply a diluted **mist coat** (50% water / 50% contract matt emulsion) first.
2. **Forgetting to Subtract Windows and Doors**: Subtract large patio doors, built-in wardrobes, and windows when measuring wall surface area. Subtracting 3 to 5 sq m for a standard window avoids over-buying paint.
3. **Dark Color to Light Color Transformations**: Transitioning from dark navy or charcoal walls to off-white or light pastel shades requires a **3rd coat** or a dedicated high-opacity white primer undercoat.
