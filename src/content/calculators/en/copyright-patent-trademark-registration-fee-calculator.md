Protecting your business brand name, logo, original invention, or industrial design in the UK is managed by the **Intellectual Property Office (UK IPO)**.

There are four primary types of **Intellectual Property (IP) Rights** in the UK: **Trade Marks** (£170 online application fee), **Patents** (£310 official fees), **Registered Designs** (£50), and **Copyright** (which applies automatically for £0 fee).

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Intellectual Property (IP) Rules for 2026/27

### 1. UK Trade Marks (Brand Names & Logos)
- **Filing Fee**: **£170.00** for an online application covering **1 Nice Class of goods or services**.
- **Additional Class Fee**: Add **+£50.00 for each additional class** selected.
- **Duration**: A registered UK trade mark lasts **10 years** and can be renewed every 10 years indefinitely (£200 renewal fee).
- *Example*: Registering a brand name in **2 classes (e.g. Class 25 Clothing + Class 35 Online Retail)**:
  - `Base 1st Class Fee = £170.00`.
  - `1 Additional Class = £50.00`.
  - `Total UK IPO Official Fee = £170 + £50 = £220.00`.

### 2. UK Patents (Inventions & Technical Innovations)
- **Filing & Application Fee**: **£60.00** online filing fee.
- **Patent Search Fee**: **£150.00** (UK IPO searches prior art worldwide).
- **Substantive Examination Fee**: **£100.00** (UK IPO examines whether the invention is novel and non-obvious).
- **Total Official Statutory Patent Fees**: **£310.00**.
- **Duration**: Monopoly protection for up to **20 years** (annual renewal fees apply after year 4).
- *Attorney Assistance*: Drafting a patent specification requires a Chartered Patent Attorney (**~£1,500 to £3,500+**).

### 3. UK Registered Designs (Visual Appearance & Shapes)
- **Official Fee**: **£50.00** for a single design applied online via the UK IPO portal.
- **Multiple Designs**: Add **+£20.00 for each additional design** included in the same application.
- **Duration**: Protects physical appearance for up to **25 years** (renewed every 5 years).

### 4. UK Copyright (Written Works, Code, Music & Art)
- Under the **Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988**, copyright protection arises **AUTOMATICALLY** upon creation of original literary, artistic, musical, or software code.
- **Statutory Fee**: **£0.00 (Free)**. There is no official UK government copyright register.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & IP Registration Warnings

1. **Failing to Conduct a Trade Mark Clearance Search Before Filing**: The UK IPO does NOT refuse a trade mark simply because an earlier conflicting mark exists. If you register a mark similar to an existing brand, the owner can file an **Opposition Proceeding**, forcing you to forfeit your £170 fee and pay the opponent's legal costs. Always conduct a free UK IPO Trade Mark Search first.
2. **Disclosing an Invention Publicly Before Filing a Patent**: Under UK patent law, an invention MUST be secret at the filing date. Demonstrating an invention on YouTube, social media, or pitching to investors without a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) invalidates your right to a patent.
3. **Assuming a UK Company House Name Gives Trade Mark Rights**: Registering a company name at Companies House or buying a .co.uk domain name does NOT grant trade mark rights. Only an official UK IPO Trade Mark Registration grants exclusive legal rights to prevent brand infringement.

    </div>

    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No. A UK Trade Mark protects your brand ONLY within the UK. To protect your brand in Europe, apply for an EU Trade Mark (EUTM via EUIPO — €850 fee) or file an international application via the WIPO Madrid System.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">

    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>If no oppositions are filed by third parties during the mandatory 2-month publication window, a UK trade mark application takes **3 to 4 months** from filing to full registration.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is Patent Box tax relief? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
