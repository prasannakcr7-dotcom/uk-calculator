Exchanging British Pounds (GBP) into foreign currencies—whether for holiday travel, overseas property purchases, international business invoices, or sending money home—involves understanding both headline exchange rates and hidden transaction margins.

While Google or XE show the real-time "mid-market" interbank exchange rate, retail providers (such as high-street banks, credit card issuers, and airport bureau de change booths) add a percentage markup to the exchange rate. This hidden markup serves as an extra fee added to your transaction.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Mid-Market Rate**: The midpoint between buying and selling prices on wholesale interbank currency markets; the true benchmark rate.
- **Retail Exchange Margins**:
  - Specialist FX Apps (e.g. Wise, Revolut): **0.3% to 0.6%** markup.
  - Standard Online Brokers: **1.0% to 1.5%** markup.
  - High Street Bank Debit/Credit Cards: **2.75% to 3.00%** non-sterling transaction fee.
  - Airport Currency Kiosks: **5.0% to 10.0%** spread markup.
- **Payment Services Regulations 2017**: Mandates transparent disclosure of total transaction fees for UK payment transfers.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Accepting Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) Abroad**: When paying by card abroad, always choose to pay in the **local currency** (EUR/USD) rather than GBP; accepting DCC allows overseas merchants to charge exorbitant 5%–8% FX rates!
- **Exchanging Cash at Airports**: Airport exchange desks charge the highest markups in the industry (often 5% to 10% worse than mid-market rates).
- **Ignoring Non-Sterling Transaction Fees**: Standard UK credit and debit cards charge a 2.99% fee on overseas purchases; use dedicated fee-free travel debit cards instead.
