The United Kingdom operates a unique **hybrid measurement system**. While metric units (metres, kilograms, litres) are legally mandated for retail trade, science, weather forecasts, and petrol sales under the **Weights and Measures Act 1985**, UK culture and road transport retain statutory UK Imperial units (miles for road distances/speed limits, pints for draught beer and milk, and feet/inches or stones/pounds for personal height and body weight).

Accurately converting between UK Imperial and Metric units requires applying official international conversion standards—including distinguishing between **UK Imperial fluid units** and **US Customary liquid units**.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Length & Height Conversions**:
  - `1 Inch = 2.54 cm` (exact standard).
  - `1 Foot = 12 Inches = 30.48 cm = 0.3048 m`.
  - `1 Metre = 3.28084 Feet = 39.3701 Inches`.
- **Weight Conversions**:
  - `1 Stone = 14 Pounds (lbs) = 6.35029 kg`.
  - `1 Pound (lb) = 16 Ounces = 0.45359237 kg`.
  - `1 Kilogram = 2.20462 lbs = 0.157473 Stones`.
- **Distance & Speed Conversions**:
  - `1 UK Statute Mile = 1,760 Yards = 1.609344 km`.
  - `1 Kilometre = 0.621371 Miles`.
- **Liquid Volume Conversions (UK Imperial)**:
  - `1 UK Imperial Pint = 20 UK Fluid Ounces = 568.26125 ml` (0.56826 L).
  - `1 UK Imperial Gallon = 8 UK Pints = 4.54609 Litres`.
  - *(Note: A US Liquid Pint is smaller, equal to 473.176 ml; a US Liquid Gallon is 3.78541 L)*.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Confusing UK Imperial Gallons with US Gallons**: A UK Imperial gallon (4.546 L) is 20% larger than a US liquid gallon (3.785 L); using US fuel conversion apps overstates fuel economy in the UK.
- **Using 1 Stone = 10 lbs Instead of 14 lbs**: A UK stone consists of exactly **14 pounds (lbs)**.
- **Forgetting That UK Road Signs Use Imperial Miles**: UK speed limits (30, 60, 70 mph) and road signs are legally specified in Imperial miles, not kilometres.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is the difference between a UK pint and a US pint?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>A UK Imperial pint contains 20 UK fluid ounces (568.26 ml), whereas a US liquid pint contains 16 US fluid ounces (473.18 ml). A UK pint is approximately 20% larger than a US pint.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Why does the UK use both Imperial and Metric units?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>The UK transitioned toward the metric system under the Metrication Board starting in 1965. However, while trade, manufacturing, and food retail were legalized in metric, public preference and road traffic laws preserved miles for roads and pints for draught beer.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How many pounds (lbs) are in a UK stone?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>There are exactly 14 pounds (lbs) in 1 UK stone. To convert a body weight of 10 stone 7 lbs to pounds: `(10 × 14) + 7 = 147 lbs` (which equals 66.68 kg).</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How do I convert kilometres per hour (km/h) to miles per hour (mph)?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>To convert km/h to mph, multiply by 0.621371 (or divide by 1.609344). For example, a European speed limit of 120 km/h equals approximately 74.5 mph in the UK.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
