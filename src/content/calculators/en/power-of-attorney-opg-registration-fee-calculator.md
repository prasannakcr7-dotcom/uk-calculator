Creating a **Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA)** is one of the most vital legal precautions an adult in the UK can take. An LPA is a legal document enabling you (the 'Donor') to appoint trusted family members or friends (the 'Attorneys') to make decisions on your behalf if you lose mental capacity in the future due to dementia, stroke, or severe illness.

To make an LPA legally enforceable, it MUST be registered with the **Office of the Public Guardian (OPG)**, incurring a statutory registration fee of **£82.00 per LPA document**.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK LPA Rules & Fee Remissions for 2026/27

### 1. The Two Types of Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA)
- **Property & Financial Affairs LPA**: Authorizes attorneys to manage bank accounts, pay bills, collect pensions, buy/sell property, and handle investments. Can be used while you still have capacity (with your permission).
- **Health & Welfare LPA**: Authorizes attorneys to make decisions regarding your daily care, medical treatment, care home selection, and life-sustaining treatment. Can ONLY be used if you lose mental capacity.
- *Most individuals make BOTH LPAs*: Making both LPAs for a single donor requires paying **2 OPG registration fees (£82 × 2 = £164.00 total)**. A couple making both LPAs requires **4 documents (£82 × 4 = £328.00 total)**.

### 2. OPG Statutory Fee Remissions & Exemptions
If the Donor has low income or receives government benefits, the OPG grants statutory fee discounts:
- **50% Fee Remission (£41.00 per LPA)**: Qualifies if the Donor's gross annual income before tax is **under £12,000.00 per year**.
- **100% Fee Exemption (£0.00 per LPA)**: Qualifies if the Donor receives qualifying means-tested benefits, including **Universal Credit**, **Income Support**, **Guarantee Credit element of Pension Credit**, or **Housing Benefit**.

### 3. DIY Online OPG Portal vs Solicitor Preparation Fees
- **DIY Online OPG Portal**: You can complete LPA forms yourself using the official GOV.UK online service for **£0 legal fees** (paying only the £82 OPG court registration fee per document).
- **Solicitor Preparation**: A solicitor charges **£350 to £500** to draft both LPAs for a single person (or **£650 to £900** for a couple), acting as the official Certificate Provider to verify mental capacity.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & LPA Warnings

1. **Waiting Until Capacity is Lost (Court of Protection Deputyship)**: Once a person loses mental capacity due to advanced dementia or stroke, it is TOO LATE to make an LPA. Relatives must instead apply to the **Court of Protection for a Deputyship Order**, which costs **£371 court fee + £500+ annual supervision fees + £1,500+ legal costs** and takes 6 to 12 months.
2. **Choosing Ineligible Certificate Providers**: An LPA requires a 'Certificate Provider' to sign confirming the donor understands the document and is not under undue pressure. A certificate provider CANNOT be a named attorney, family member, or business partner.
3. **Failing to Register the LPA Immediately**: An unregistered LPA cannot be used by attorneys. Registering LPAs with the OPG immediately after signing takes **12 to 16 weeks**, ensuring the document is ready for instant use when needed.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<div class="faq-sec">
  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is the difference between Jointly and Jointly & Severally appointed attorneys? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Appointing attorneys 'Jointly' means all attorneys MUST agree and sign every single document together (if one dies, the LPA fails). Appointing 'Jointly & Severally' allows attorneys to act either together or independently, providing far greater practical flexibility.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Can an Attorney use an LPA to alter a donor's Will? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No! An attorney operating under an LPA has zero legal authority to alter, amend, or rewrite a donor's Will or make substantial gifts from the donor's estate.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How do banks verify an LPA for financial management? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Once registered, the OPG issues a secure digital **Use an LPA Access Code**. Attorneys provide this 16-character code to UK banks and utility companies to instantly verify their legal authority online.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What happens if an LPA document contains an error during registration? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>If the OPG rejects an LPA due to a clerical error, a repeat application submitted within 3 months costs a reduced re-application fee of **£41.00**.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
