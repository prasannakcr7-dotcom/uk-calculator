**Shared Ownership** is a UK government-backed affordable homeownership scheme designed to help first-time buyers buy a share of a residential property (typically **10% to 75%** of full market value) using a cash deposit and mortgage, while paying discounted rent to a **Housing Association** on the remaining unowned equity share.

Because Shared Ownership involves three distinct monthly outlays—**Mortgage Repayments**, **Housing Association Rent**, and **Leasehold Service Charges**—calculating your total combined monthly commitment is essential before submitting a buyer application.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Housing Association Rent Formula**:
  - `Unowned Equity Share (£) = Full Market Value × (100% - Purchased Share %)`.
  - `Annual Rent (£) = Unowned Equity Share × Housing Association Rent Rate (typically 2.75% per year)`.
  - `Monthly Rent (£) = Annual Rent / 12`.
- **Annual Rent Increases**: Rent increases annually in April, capped by government guidelines at **RPI + 0.5%** or CPI + 1.0%.
- **Leasehold Service Charges**: Covers building insurance, communal maintenance, groundskeeping, and reserve sinking funds. On 100% shared ownership leaseholds, service charges remain 100% the leaseholder's responsibility regardless of share percentage owned.
- **Staircasing**: The process of buying additional shares (in increments as small as 1% or 10%) up to full 100% outright ownership. Buying 100% of the property eliminates housing association rent entirely.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Forgetting That Service Charges Apply in Full**: You pay 100% of the building service charge and ground rent, even if you only own a 25% equity share.
- **Underestimating Annual Rent Escalation**: Housing association rents increase every April linked to RPI inflation; over 10 years, unowned share rent can rise significantly.
- **Ignoring Valuation Fees When Staircasing**: Buying extra equity shares requires paying for a RICS chartered surveyor valuation and conveyancing legal fees each time you staircase.
