Maintaining your vision in the UK requires combining **sight tests (eye examinations)**, **prescription glasses (frames and lenses)**, and **contact lens care schemes**.

Whether you wear **daily disposable contact lenses (£28/month)** or replace designer **varifocal glasses (£380 every 2 years)**, calculating your total annual optical care expenses—and checking **NHS Optical Voucher eligibility**—helps you optimize your eyewear budget.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Optical Rules & NHS Vouchers for 2026/27

### 1. NHS Free Eye Examination Eligibility
A standard private sight test in the UK costs **£25 to £35**. However, you are legally entitled to a **100% Free NHS Sight Test** if you:
- Are aged **under 16** (or aged **16-18 in full-time education**).
- Are aged **60 or over**.
- Have been diagnosed with **diabetes** or **glaucoma** (or aged 40+ with a direct family history of glaucoma).
- Are registered blind or partially sighted.
- Receive qualifying income benefits (Universal Credit, Income Support, Guarantee Credit) or hold an **NHS HC2 Certificate**.
- *Scotland Exception*: All Scottish residents receive **100% free NHS eye examinations** regardless of age.

### 2. NHS GOS Optical Vouchers (Glasses Help)
If you qualify for an NHS free sight test based on low income, age (under 19 in education), or complex prescriptions, you receive an **NHS Optical Voucher (GOS Voucher)** toward the cost of glasses or contact lenses:
- **Voucher A (Single Vision Lenses)**: **£41.70**.
- **Voucher B (Single Vision High Prescription)**: **£63.00**.
- **Voucher E (Bifocal Lenses)**: **£72.00**.
- **Voucher H (Complex / Prism / Varifocal Lenses)**: Up to **£225.00**.

### 3. Contact Lens Subscriptions & Eye Health Rules
- **Daily Disposable Lenses (~£25 to £35/month)**: Maximum convenience and hygiene (fresh lens every day, zero cleaning solution required).
- **Monthly Reusable Lenses (~£12 to £18/month)**: Lower cost, but requires purchasing multi-purpose disinfecting contact lens solution (~£6/mo).
- **Contact Lens Check-Ups**: UK law requires contact lens wearers to undergo a contact lens fitting/check-up at least once every 12 to 24 months before opticians can legally re-issue contact lens prescriptions.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Optical Eyewear Warnings

1. **Overpaying for High-Index Thin Lenses**: Strong prescriptions (-4.00D or higher) benefit from 1.67 or 1.74 high-index thin lenses. High street opticians often mark up thin lenses by £100+, whereas online prescription glazing services fit high-index lenses for half the price.
2. **Sleeping in Standard Contact Lenses**: Never sleep in standard hydrogel contact lenses unless specifically prescribed continuous-wear extended-life lenses. Sleeping in standard lenses starves the cornea of oxygen, creating severe microbial keratitis eye infections.
3. **Buying Contact Lenses Without a Valid Specification**: UK law mandates that contact lenses can only be sold to customers holding an in-date contact lens specification issued by a registered optometrist.
