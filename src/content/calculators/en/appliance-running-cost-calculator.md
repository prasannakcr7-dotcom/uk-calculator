With UK electricity rates regulated under the **Ofgem Energy Price Cap**, understanding how much individual household appliances cost to run per hour, per wash cycle, or per year is essential for managing domestic utility bills.

From high-power heating devices like **electric showers (9,000W)** and **tumble dryers (2,500W)** to continuous appliances like **fridge freezers**, calculating exact **Kilowatt-Hour (kWh)** consumption helps households identify energy-guzzling appliances.

## ⚙️ Statutory Appliance Energy Calculations for 2026/27

### 1. The Kilowatt-Hour (kWh) Appliance Formula
To calculate how much electricity an appliance consumes and its financial running cost:
- **Kilowatt-Hours Consumed**: `kWh = [ Power (Watts) × Usage Time (Hours) ] ÷ 1,000`.
- **Financial Running Cost**: `Cost (£) = kWh Consumed × Unit Rate (p/kWh) ÷ 100`.
- *Example*: Running a **2,500 Watt tumble dryer** for **1.5 hours** at a **24.5p/kWh unit rate**:
  - `kWh = (2,500 × 1.5) ÷ 1,000 = 3.75 kWh`.
  - `Cost = (3.75 × 24.5p) ÷ 100 = £0.919 per cycle (£335.34/year if used daily)`.

### 2. UK Household Appliance Power Spectrum
- **High Energy Power Devices (2,000W to 9,000W)**:
  - Electric Shower: **8,500W – 10,500W** (Costs ~£2.20 per 60 mins / ~37p for a 10-min shower).
  - Electric Kettle: **3,000W** (Costs ~1.2p per boil / ~7.35p per hour).
  - Tumble Dryer: **2,500W** (Costs ~61.3p per hour).
  - Fan Heater / Electric Oven: **2,000W** (Costs ~49.0p per hour).
- **Medium Energy Devices (200W to 1,500W)**:
  - Air Fryer: **1,500W** (Costs ~36.8p per hour).
  - Washing Machine: **200W average** (Costs ~4.9p per hour / ~35p per hot wash cycle).
- **Low / Continuous Energy Devices (10W to 150W)**:
  - Fridge Freezer: **150W continuous compressor average** (Costs ~88p per day / ~£320 per year).
  - 65" LED TV / Gaming Console: **100W** (Costs ~2.5p per hour).
  - LED Light Bulb: **10W** (Costs ~0.25p per hour).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Appliance Cost Warnings

1. **Phantom Standby Power Waste**: Household electronics left on standby (TVs, microwave displays, games consoles, phone chargers) consume 'vampire power'. Switching standby devices off at the wall saves average UK households **£45 to £65 per year**.
2. **Ignoring Heat Pump Tumble Dryers**: Traditional condenser tumble dryers (2,500W) cost over 60p/hr to run. Modern **Heat Pump Tumble Dryers** (900W) use 60% less electricity, saving over £180/yr in busy family homes.
3. **Washing Clothes at 60°C vs 30°C**: Heating water accounts for **90% of a washing machine's energy consumption**. Washing laundry at 30°C instead of 60°C reduces electricity usage per wash cycle by over 50%.
