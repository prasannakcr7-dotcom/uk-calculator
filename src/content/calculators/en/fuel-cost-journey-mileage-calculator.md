Calculating the **total fuel cost of a UK road trip** requires bridging the gap between UK measurement systems: UK roads display distance in **miles** and car dashboards display efficiency in **Miles Per Gallon (MPG)**, but petrol and diesel pumps sold at petrol stations are priced in **pence per litre**.

Because 1 UK Imperial Gallon equals **4.54609 Litres**, calculating exact journey fuel costs requires converting miles traveled into imperial gallons, converting gallons into litres, and multiplying by current pump prices.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **UK Imperial Gallon Standard**: `1 UK Imperial Gallon = 4.54609 Litres`.
- **Fuel Volume Formula**:
  `Total Gallons Required = Journey Miles / Vehicle MPG`.
  `Total Litres Required = Total Gallons × 4.54609`.
- **Total Journey Fuel Cost Formula**:
  `Total Cost (£) = Total Litres × (Pence Per Litre / 100)`.
- **Pence Per Mile Formula**:
  `Cost Per Mile (p) = (Total Cost in Pounds × 100) / Journey Miles`.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Confusing UK Imperial Gallons with US Gallons**: A US gallon is smaller (3.785 L); using US MPG figures understates your vehicle's true UK fuel consumption efficiency by 20%!
- **Assuming Manufacturer WLTP MPG Matches Real-World Driving**: Official WLTP lab figures often overstate real-world fuel economy by 10% to 15%, especially during urban stop-start driving.
- **Forgetting Additional Toll and Clean Air Charges**: A road trip cost calculation must also include motorway tolls (e.g. M6 Toll), bridge crossings (Dartford Crossing), or Clean Air Zone charges (London ULEZ).
