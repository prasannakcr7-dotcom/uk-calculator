Driving across key UK road networks often involves crossing **toll motorways, estuarial tunnels, and bridges**.

From Britain's primary privatized motorway—the **M6 Toll bypass (£9.70 car rate)**—to barrierless free-flow video tolling at the **Dartford Crossing (£2.50 Dart Charge)**, setting up **pre-paid electronic tag accounts** saves regular commuters **20% on crossing fees**.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Toll Road & Crossing Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Major UK Toll Locations & Standard Rates
- **M6 Toll Motorway (West Midlands Bypass)**:
  - *Cars / Small Vans (Class 2)*: **£9.70** (Day rate 07:00-19:00) / **£8.70** (Night rate).
  - *Heavy Goods Vehicles (Class 5 HGV)*: **£16.50** per passage.
- **Dartford Crossing / Dart Charge (A282 M25 East)**:
  - *Cars (Class 2)*: **£2.50** per crossing (06:00 to 22:00). Free 22:00 to 06:00.
  - *Motorcycles*: **FREE**.
  - *Pre-paid Account Rate*: **£2.00 per crossing (20% discount)**.
- **Mersey Gateway & Silver Jubilee Bridges (Runcorn/Widnes)**:
  - *Cars*: **£2.00** per crossing (Pre-paid account rate **£1.80**).
- **Tyne Tunnels (Newcastle A19)**:
  - *Cars*: **£2.40** per crossing (Pre-paid TT2 account rate **£2.16**).

### 2. Barrierless Free-Flow Video Tolling Rules
- **No Toll Booth Barriers**: Modern UK crossings like Dartford, Mersey Gateway, and Tyne Tunnel use ANPR camera recognition instead of physical toll booths.
- **Payment Deadline**: Toll payments MUST be made online, by phone, or at Payzone outlets by **midnight on the day following the crossing**.
- **Penalty Charge Notice (PCN)**: Failing to pay on time results in a **£70 PCN** (reduced to £35 if paid within 14 days, plus the original toll).

### 3. Toll-Free Bridges in Wales & Scotland
- **Severn River Bridges (M4 / M48 Wales)**: Statutory tolls were permanently **ABOLISHED** in December 2018 (now 100% free to cross into Wales).
- **Forth Road Bridge & Tay Road Bridge (Scotland)**: Statutory tolls were permanently **ABOLISHED** by the Scottish Government in 2008.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Toll Road Warnings

1. **Forgetting Free-Flow Toll Deadlines**: Visiting London or Newcastle and driving across Dartford or Tyne Tunnels without a pre-paid account easily slips your mind. Always set a phone reminder to pay online before midnight the next day.
2. **Expired Payment Cards on Auto-Pay Accounts**: If your credit/debit card expires, automatic toll top-ups fail. Toll operators will immediately issue £70 PCN penalty notices for unpaid crossings.
3. **Driving Heavy Vans on Car Rates**: Commercial vans exceeding 3,500kg gross vehicle weight or twin-rear-wheel vans are classified under HGV toll rates, incurring double or triple the standard car rate.
