Installing rooftop **Solar PV panels** combined with **lithium battery storage** (e.g. 5.0 kWh to 13.5 kWh Tesla Powerwall) is one of the most effective ways for UK homeowners to shield against volatile Grid electricity tariffs.

With the UK government extending the **0% VAT rate incentive** on residential solar PV and energy storage systems, combining daytime solar generation with evening battery discharge increases self-consumption from **35% up to 80%**, achieving payback periods of **7 to 10 years**.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Solar PV & Battery Storage Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Solar Generation Yields & 0% VAT Incentive
- **UK Generation Yield**: In the UK, 1 kWp of south-facing solar PV panels generates approximately **850 to 950 kWh of electricity per year**.
  - A standard **4.0 kWp array (10 to 12 panels)** generates ~**3,400 kWh per year**.
- **0% VAT Incentive**: Residential solar PV panels and standalone home battery storage installations benefit from a **0% VAT rate** (zero-rated for VAT).

### 2. Battery Storage Self-Consumption Multiplier
- **Solar PV Only (No Battery)**: Without a battery, daytime solar energy is generated when occupants are at work. Average self-consumption is only **35%**, with 65% exported to the grid.
- **Solar PV + Battery Storage (5.0 kWh+)**: Storing daytime surplus solar energy in a battery for evening use boosts self-consumption to **75% – 85%**, dramatically lowering grid electricity purchases.

### 3. Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) Tariffs
Under Ofgem SEG regulations, licensed UK energy suppliers pay households for surplus solar electricity exported back to the National Grid:
- **SEG Export Rates**: Major suppliers offer fixed or dynamic export tariffs ranging from **12.0p to 15.0p+ per kWh exported**.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Solar PV Warnings

1. **Failing to Choose an MCS Certified Installer**: To qualify for the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) tariff and 0% VAT, your solar PV array and battery system MUST be installed by an **MCS (Microgeneration Certification Scheme) certified installer**.
2. **Ignoring Roof Orientation and Shading**: South-facing roofs at a 30° to 40° pitch produce 100% maximum yield. East/West roofs yield ~80% output. Shading from chimney stacks or trees severely reduces string inverter performance (use micro-inverters or Tigo optimizers for shaded roofs).
3. **G98 / G99 Grid Connection Notifications**: Solar inverters rated up to 3.68 kW per phase require a simple post-install **G98 notification** to the DNO (Distribution Network Operator). Inverters above 3.68 kW require prior **G99 DNO approval** before installation.
