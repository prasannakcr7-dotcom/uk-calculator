Planning a **single-story rear kitchen extension**, **two-story side extension**, or **wraparound extension** in the UK requires establishing a realistic **construction budget per square metre (sq m)**.

According to **RICS BCIS data**, average UK house extension build costs range from **£1,800 to £2,500 per square metre** for basic to standard finishes (rising to **£3,000+ per sq m in Greater London**).

## ⚙️ UK House Extension Cost Benchmarks per Sq M for 2026/27

### 1. Extension Type Cost Comparison (£ per sq m)
- **Single-Story Rear / Side Extension**: **£1,800 to £2,400 per sq m** (Basic to High-End finish).
- **Two-Story Extension**: **£1,500 to £2,000 per sq m** (Economies of scale apply: foundations and roof structure costs are shared across two levels).
- **Wraparound Extension (Rear + Side L-Shape)**: **£2,200 to £2,800 per sq m** (Requires significant structural steelwork to support upper floors).
- **Basement Excavation Extension**: **£2,800 to £4,000+ per sq m** (Requires extensive underpinning, tanking, and structural engineering).

### 2. Regional UK Location Multipliers
Labor and subcontractor rates vary significantly across the UK:
- **Greater London & South East England**: Add a **+25% to +35% regional cost premium**.
- **Midlands & East Anglia**: **1.0x (National Average)**.
- **North of England, Wales & Scotland**: Apply a **-10% regional discount**.

### 3. Comprehensive Project Budget Split
A realistic extension budget allocates funds across three main areas:
- **Main Shell & Core Construction**: **70% of total budget** (foundations, brickwork, roof, doors/windows).
- **Internal Fit-Out & Plumbing**: **20% of total budget** (plastering, kitchen/bathroom fit-out, electrics, heating).
- **Professional Fees & Statutory Charges**: **10% of total budget** (architect fees, structural engineer, building control, party wall).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Extension Budget Warnings

1. **Forgetting 20% VAT on Contractor Quotes**: Builder quotes are frequently provided net of VAT. Adding **20% VAT** to a £50,000 builder quote adds £10,000 to your final bill. Always confirm if quotes are inclusive of VAT.
2. **Underestimating Structural Steel Costs**: Opening up the entire rear wall of a house for bi-fold doors requires heavy structural steel RSJ frames (goalpost frames). Steelwork and crane hire can easily add £4,000 to £8,000 to structural costs.
3. **Failing to Hold a 10% Cash Contingency**: Unforeseen ground conditions (such as discovering unmapped sewer pipes, soft clay, or deep tree roots requiring 2.5m trench foundations) will consume your 10% contingency reserve quickly.
