Under the **Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018**, the Scottish Government devolved carer welfare benefits away from the DWP to **Social Security Scotland**, replacing UK Carer's Allowance with the **Carer Support Payment**.

To recognise the vital contribution of unpaid carers, Social Security Scotland pays an extra devolved benefit called the **Carer's Allowance Supplement (CAS)**. This supplement consists of **two automatic lump-sum payments per year** (paid in June and December), raising total carer benefit income in Scotland significantly above the rest of the UK.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Eligibility Criteria for Carer's Allowance Supplement (CAS)**:
  - Must live in Scotland on the qualifying dates (typically mid-April for June payment and mid-October for December payment).
  - Must be in receipt of Scottish Carer Support Payment or Carer's Allowance on the qualifying date.
  - Must provide at least **35 hours of care per week** for a disabled person receiving qualifying disability benefits (ADP, PIP, DLA, or AA).
- **Bi-Annual Payment Schedule (2026/27)**:
  - **June Payment**: **£288.60** lump sum.
  - **December Payment**: **£288.60** lump sum.
  - Total Annual Supplement Top-Up: **£577.20 / year**.
- **Weekly Base Carer Benefit**: £81.90 per week (£4,258.80 / year).
- **Total Annual Scottish Carer Income**: `Base Benefit (£4,258.80) + CAS Supplement (£577.20) = £4,836.00 / year` (effective weekly income of **£93.00 / week**).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Moving Out of Scotland Before Qualifying Dates**: Moving to England or Wales before the April or October qualifying dates forfeits that 6-month supplement payment.
- **Exceeding the Weekly Net Earnings Cap**: Earning more than the statutory net earnings limit (£151/week after allowable care and pension deductions) cancels entitlement to base Carer Support Payment, automatically cancelling CAS.
- **Failing to Claim Overlapping Benefits Correctly**: Carer Support Payment interacts with Universal Credit via the Carer Element (£198.31/mo), which can be claimed alongside CAS.
