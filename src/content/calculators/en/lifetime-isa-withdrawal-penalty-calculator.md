While the **Lifetime ISA (LISA)** offers a lucrative **25% government bonus** on savings, UK tax legislation imposes a strict **25% statutory withdrawal penalty** if you withdraw money for unauthorized reasons.

Understanding why a **25% penalty on the total withdrawn balance** results in losing **6.25% of your original cash deposits** helps savers avoid unexpected financial losses.

## ⚙️ Statutory 25% LISA Penalty Math & Rules for 2026/27

### 1. The 25% Government Penalty Formula
If you withdraw funds before age 60 for any purpose other than purchasing a qualifying first home (valued up to £450,000):
- **Statutory Penalty**: Your LISA provider automatically deducts **25% of the total amount withdrawn**.
- **Net Cash Received**: You receive **75%** of your current LISA balance in your bank account.

### 2. The Net 6.25% Own Cash Loss Explanation
Many savers mistakenly believe the 25% penalty simply "takes back the 25% bonus". However, because the 25% penalty is applied to the **new larger balance (deposit + bonus)**, it reclaims more than the original bonus:
- *Example*: You deposit **£1,000 of your own money**.
- HMRC adds a 25% bonus (**+£250**), making your total balance **£1,250**.
- Making an unauthorized withdrawal incurs a 25% penalty on £1,250 = **-£312.50 Penalty**.
- You receive **£937.50 in your bank account**.
- Net Result: You lost **£62.50 (6.25%)** of your original £1,000 cash deposit.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Penalty Warnings

1. **Exceeding the £450,000 Property Price Cap**: If you use your LISA to purchase a first home priced at £450,001 or more, HMRC treats the withdrawal as unauthorized, triggering the full 25% penalty on your entire home deposit.
2. **Withdrawing Before Holding Account for 12 Months**: You MUST make your initial LISA deposit at least **12 months before** using the account for a property purchase. Withdrawing earlier triggers the 25% penalty.
3. **Emergency Temporary Suspensions**: During emergency crises, HM Treasury has occasionally reduced the LISA penalty temporarily to 20% (simply taking back the bonus). However, the standard statutory penalty is **25%**.
