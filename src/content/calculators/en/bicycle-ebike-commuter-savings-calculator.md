Switching your daily commute from a car, bus, or train to a **push bike or electric e-Bike** is one of the fastest ways to save money, improve cardiovascular health, and reduce carbon emissions in the UK.

Combining replaced car fuel/parking expenses with the statutory **HMRC Cycle to Work Scheme** allows UK employees to save **32% to 42% off the retail price** of a new bicycle or e-Bike package, recovering the bike purchase cost within **6 to 12 months**.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Cycle to Work Rules & EAPC Regulations for 2026/27

### 1. How the HMRC Cycle to Work Scheme Works
- **Salary Sacrifice Mechanism**: Your employer purchases the bike package on your behalf. You repay the net cost via monthly deductions from your **gross pre-tax salary** over a 12-month or 24-month hire period.
- **Tax & National Insurance Savings**: Because deductions are taken before tax and NI:
  - *Basic Rate Taxpayer (20% Tax + 8% NI)*: **Saves 32%** off the retail price.
  - *Higher Rate Taxpayer (40% Tax + 2% NI)*: **Saves 42%** off the retail price.
  - *Additional Rate Taxpayer (45% Tax + 2% NI)*: **Saves 47%** off the retail price.
- *Example*: Purchasing a **£1,500 e-Bike package** as a **40% Higher Rate Taxpayer**:
  - `Scheme Tax Savings = £1,500 × 42% = £630.00 discount`.
  - `Net Out-of-Pocket Cost = £1,500 – £630 = £870.00 (£72.50 per month)`.

### 2. Electrically Assisted Pedal Cycles (EAPC) Legal Rules
Electric e-Bikes offer motorized pedal assistance, enabling effortless hill climbing and longer commutes without sweating. Under UK **EAPC Regulations**:
- **Maximum Motor Power**: Continuous rated output MUST NOT exceed **250 Watts**.
- **Maximum Assist Speed**: Electric motor assistance MUST cut out when speed reaches **15.5 mph (25 km/h)**.
- **No License or Tax Required**: EAPCs meeting these criteria are legally classified as standard pedal cycles (no driving license, road tax, or helmet law required for riders aged 14+).

### 3. Calculating Travel Savings
- Commuting by car or public transport costs an average of **50p to 70p per mile** (fuel, parking, transit tickets).
- Cycling a **6-mile one-way commute (12 miles/day)** 4 days a week (192 miles/month) saves **£1,150+ per year** in travel expenses.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Cycle Commuter Warnings

1. **Choosing Cheap Cable Locks for City Parking**: Bike theft is widespread in major UK towns. Always invest in a **Sold Secure Gold or Diamond rated D-lock** to secure both the frame and back wheel to a fixed cycle stand.
2. **Forgetting End of Hire Ownership Fees**: At the end of the 12-month salary sacrifice period, HMRC requires a nominal 'Fair Market Value' ownership transfer fee (typically 3% to 7% of original value if extended to a 4-year small benefit loan).
3. **Buying an Incompatible e-Bike (Speed Pedelecs)**: High-speed e-Bikes with motors over 250W or throttle speeds over 15.5 mph are legally classified as mopeds, requiring license plates, helmet, insurance, and DVLA registration.
