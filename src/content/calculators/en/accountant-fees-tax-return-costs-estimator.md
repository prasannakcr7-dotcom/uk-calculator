Hiring a professional chartered accountant or tax advisor ensures full HMRC compliance, timely statutory filings, and maximum tax efficiency for your UK business.

Understanding average UK **accountancy fee structures**—and how **100% tax deductibility** lowers net costs—helps business owners select the right service package.

## ⚙️ Standard UK Accountancy Market Fees for 2026/27

### 1. Sole Trader Accountancy Fees
- **Basic Self Assessment Tax Return**: **£150 to £350 per year** (Simple tax return with income/expense summary).
- **Full Sole Trader Accounts & Tax Return**: **£400 to £700 per year** (Includes statutory balance sheet, income statement, and Self Assessment filing).

### 2. Limited Company Accountancy Fees
- **Micro-Entity Accounts & Corporation Tax Return (CT600)**: **£900 to £1,500 per year** (£75 to £125/month).
- **Full Comprehensive Package (Accounts + MTD VAT + PAYE Payroll)**: **£1,800 to £3,000 per year** (£150 to £250/month).

### 3. Tax Deductibility Rules
Professional accountancy fees incurred for preparing business accounts, corporate tax returns, VAT returns, and payroll are **100% allowable business expenses**:
- **Sole Traders**: Deducted against self-employed trading profits, saving 20% to 40% Income Tax and Class 4 NI.
- **Limited Companies**: Deducted against gross profits, saving 19% to 25% Corporation Tax.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Accountancy Service Warnings

1. **Unregulated Accountants**: Unlike solicitors, anyone in the UK can legally call themselves an "accountant". Always hire a qualified accountant holding credentials from a recognized UK body such as **ICAEW (ACA/FCA)**, **ACCA**, or **AAT**.
2. **Hidden Charges for Softwares**: Check whether monthly accounting software subscriptions (such as Xero or QuickBooks) are included in your accountant's fixed fee or billed as separate add-ons (£25 to £40/month extra).
3. **Personal vs Business Tax Returns**: For limited company directors, preparing personal Self Assessment tax returns is often billed separately (£150 to £250/year) from the main company accounting retainer.
