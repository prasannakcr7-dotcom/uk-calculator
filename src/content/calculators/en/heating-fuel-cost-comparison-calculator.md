Space heating accounts for over **60% of total domestic energy consumption** in UK homes.

Comparing annual space heating costs across different fuel sources—such as **Mains Natural Gas**, **Heating Oil (Kerosene)**, **LPG**, **Direct Electric**, and **Air Source Heat Pumps (ASHP)**—allows homeowners and landlords to make informed heating upgrade decisions.

## ⚙️ Statutory Fuel Efficiency & Unit Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Typical Fuel Unit Rates (Pence per kWh Delivered)
- **Mains Natural Gas**: **~6.2p per kWh** (The most economical traditional fossil fuel).
- **Heating Oil / Kerosene**: **~7.5p per kWh** (Off-grid oil tank supply).
- **LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas)**: **~9.0p per kWh** (Off-grid bulk tank or bottles).
- **Direct Electric Heating**: **~24.5p per kWh** (Panel heaters, electric radiators, storage heaters).

### 2. System Efficiency & Coefficient of Performance (COP)
The actual running cost depends heavily on system conversion efficiency:
- **Modern Condensing Gas / Oil Boilers**: **85% to 90% Efficiency** (10% to 15% energy lost through flue gases).
- **Direct Electric Resistance**: **100% Efficiency** (1 kWh electricity produces 1 kWh heat).
- **Air Source Heat Pump (ASHP)**: **300% to 350% Efficiency (COP 3.2)**. By extracting ambient heat from outdoor air, 1 kWh of electricity produces **3.2 kWh of space heat**, dramatically lowering running costs despite higher electricity unit rates.

## 📊 Practical Heating Fuel Cost Worked Examples

Below are two worked calculation examples illustrating annual space heating costs for a typical 12,000 kWh demand:

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 1: Medium home (12,000 kWh demand) heated by a 90% efficient Mains Gas Boiler</strong>
  <ul>

  <div class="net-highlight">Estimated Annual Heating Cost: **£826.67 / year** (£68.89 per month)</div>
</div>

<div class="example-box">

    <li>Gross Electricity Purchased: 12,000 ÷ 3.20 = **3,750 kWh**</li>
    <li>Electricity Unit Rate: **24.5p / kWh**</li>
  </ul>

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Heating Fuel Warnings

1. **Direct Electric Resistance Trap**: Heating a home with direct electric panel heaters or portable radiators is by far the most expensive heating method (~£2,940/yr for 12,000 kWh demand), costing 3.5 times more than mains gas.
2. **Boiler Maintenance & Scaling Losses**: Older non-condensing gas boilers operate at only 65% to 75% efficiency due to heat exchanger corrosion and limescale buildup, raising fuel bills by 25%.
3. **Boiler Upgrade Scheme (£7,500 Grant)**: Homeowners in England and Wales switching from fossil fuel boilers (gas, oil, LPG) to an Air Source Heat Pump qualify for a statutory **£7,500 government grant** under the Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<div class="faq-sec">
  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is Coefficient of Performance (COP) in heat pumps? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>COP measures heat pump efficiency. A COP of 3.2 means the heat pump generates 3.2 units of heat energy for every 1 unit of electrical energy consumed from the national grid.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Is heating oil cheaper than mains gas in the UK? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Heating oil per kWh is often comparable to gas, but oil prices fluctuate wildly based on global crude markets. Oil also requires bulk up-front tank refills of 500 to 1,000 litres.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How much insulation is required before installing a heat pump? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Heat pumps operate at lower flow temperatures (45°C to 55°C) than gas boilers (60°C to 70°C). Good loft (300mm) and cavity wall insulation are essential for heat pumps to run efficiently.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is the annual cost difference between LPG and Mains Gas? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>LPG costs approximately 40% to 50% more than mains natural gas per kWh delivered, plus annual bulk tank rental fees (~£70 to £100/year).</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
