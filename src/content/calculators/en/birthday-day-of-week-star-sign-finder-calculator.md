## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Chinese New Year Date Differences**: The Chinese Lunar New Year changes annually between late January and mid-February; individuals born in January or early February may belong to the previous year's Chinese animal sign.
- **Zodiac Cusp Ambiguity**: Dates near sign boundaries (e.g. 22/23 August) depend on the exact sun position time for that specific birth year.
- **Forgetting Historical Calendar Shifts**: UK calendar dates prior to September 1752 followed the Julian calendar rather than the modern Gregorian calendar.
