Receiving an annual work bonus is a rewarding career milestone. However, many UK employees are shocked when their net bonus payout is significantly smaller than expected.

Because a lump-sum bonus is stacked on top of your regular salary during a single PAYE pay period, it is subject to your **highest marginal tax rate**, National Insurance, and Student Loan deductions.

## ⚙️ How HMRC Taxes Bonuses for 2026/27

### 1. Marginal PAYE Income Tax & NI Rates
A bonus does not receive a separate tax-free allowance. It is taxed at your top marginal tax bracket:
- **Basic Rate Earner (Salary under £50,270)**: Subject to **20% Income Tax + 8% Class 1 NI** = **28% statutory deduction** (retaining 72p per £1 bonus).
- **Higher Rate Earner (Salary £50,271 to £100,000)**: Subject to **40% Income Tax + 2% Class 1 NI** = **42% statutory deduction** (retaining 58p per £1 bonus).
- **Personal Allowance Taper Zone (£100,000 to £125,140)**: Subject to **60% Income Tax + 2% Class 1 NI** = **62% statutory deduction** (retaining 38p per £1 bonus).

### 2. Student Loan Repayments on Bonuses
If you hold a student loan, the bonus triggers an extra deduction on earnings above threshold:
- **Plan 1 / Plan 2 / Plan 4 / Plan 5**: **9% extra deduction**.
- **Postgraduate Loan**: **6% extra deduction**.
- *Example*: A higher-rate earner with Plan 2 student loan faces a **51% marginal deduction** (40% tax + 2% NI + 9% student loan).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Bonus Sacrifice Strategies

1. **Temporary Emergency Over-Taxation in Paycheck Month**: If a £10,000 bonus is paid in a single monthly paycheck, your employer's PAYE software may temporarily multiply that month's earnings by 12, assuming you earn £120,000/year and over-deducting tax. HMRC automatically reconciles this in subsequent paychecks.
2. **Bonus Sacrifice into Pension (The 100% Tax Save)**: You can request your employer to pay part or all of your bonus directly into your workplace pension via **Bonus Sacrifice**. You avoid paying 40% Income Tax, 2% NI, and 9% Student Loan, boosting your pension pot by 100% of the gross bonus value.
3. **Loss of Tax-Free Childcare & Personal Allowance**: A large annual bonus that pushes your annual net income over £100,000 causes you to lose 100% of your 30 free childcare hours and tapers your £12,570 Personal Allowance.
