Installing **laminate, engineered wood, or solid hardwood flooring** in your home requires calculating the exact number of **flooring packs** or **boxes** to purchase.

Because laminate and timber planks are sold in sealed factory packs (typically containing **1.80 to 2.40 square metres per pack**), calculating total room square meterage plus a **10% cutting waste allowance** ensures you buy complete unopened packs without running out of planks mid-installation.

## ⚙️ Industry Timber & Laminate Flooring Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Flooring Pack Quantity Formula
To calculate how many full packs of laminate or engineered wood flooring to purchase:
- **Gross Area Required**: `Gross Area (sq m) = Net Room Area (sq m) × (1 + Waste Percentage ÷ 100)`.
- **Packs Required**: `Total Packs = Ceiling[ Gross Area (sq m) ÷ Pack Coverage (sq m/pack) ]`.
- *Example*: Fitting a **25.0 sq m room** using laminate packs covering **2.20 sq m per pack** with a **10% waste allowance**:
  - `Gross Area = 25.0 × 1.10 = 27.50 sq m`.
  - `Total Packs = Ceiling[ 27.50 ÷ 2.20 ] = Ceiling[ 12.50 ] = 13 Packs (28.60 sq m total)`.

### 2. Recommended Cutting Waste Allowances
Plank offcuts are generated at wall ends, doorways, and radiator pipes:
- **Straight Plank Laying (Parallel to Light Source)**: Add a **10% waste allowance**.
- **Diagonal Laying (45-degree Angle)**: Add a **15% waste allowance**.
- **Herringbone / Chevron Engineered Wood**: Add a **15% to 20% waste allowance** due to left and right-hand tongue-and-groove starter cuts.

### 3. Expansion Gap & Acclimatisation Rules
- **10mm Perimeter Expansion Gap**: Wood and laminate expand and contract with ambient humidity changes. A **10mm to 12mm expansion gap** must be left around all wall perimeters, hidden under skirting boards or scotia beading.
- **48-Hour Acclimatisation**: Store unopened laminate packs flat in the installation room for at least **48 hours** prior to laying to allow moisture stabilization.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Flooring Warnings

1. **Forgetting Underlay & Damp Proof Membrane (DPM)**: Laminate installed over concrete subfloors MUST have an underlay with an integrated **DPM vapour barrier** to prevent ground moisture from warping planks.
2. **Subfloor Unevenness Tolerance**: Subfloors must be flat within **3mm over a 2-metre straight edge**. Laying click-lock laminate on uneven floorboards causes click joints to snap under foot traffic.
3. **Plank Stagger Distance**: Always stagger end joints of adjacent plank rows by at least **300mm** to ensure structural stability and visual appeal.
