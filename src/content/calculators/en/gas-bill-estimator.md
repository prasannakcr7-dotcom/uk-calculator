In the UK, mains natural gas is the primary fuel used for central heating and hot water in over 80% of homes.

Understanding how your **gas meter reading (m³ or ft³)** converts to **kilowatt-hours (kWh)**, combined with your **unit rate (pence/kWh)** and **daily standing charge**, allows UK households to estimate gas bills accurately.

## ⚙️ Statutory Gas Bill Calculations & Benchmarks for 2026/27

### 1. Ofgem Typical Domestic Gas Consumption Values (TDCVs)
Ofgem establishes standard annual gas consumption benchmarks for UK properties:
- **Low Gas Usage (1-2 Bedroom Flat)**: **7,500 kWh per year**.
- **Medium Gas Usage (3-Bedroom House)**: **11,500 kWh per year**.
- **High Gas Usage (4+ Bedroom House)**: **17,000 kWh per year**.

### 2. Converting Gas Meter Units to kWh
Gas meters measure volume (cubic metres `m³` or older imperial cubic feet `ft³`), which is converted to energy (kWh) using the statutory formula:
- **Metric Meter (m³)**: `kWh = Volume (m³) × Volume Correction Factor (1.02264) × Calorific Value (~39.5) ÷ 3.6`. (Rough rule of thumb: Multiply `m³` by **11.2** to get `kWh`).
- **Imperial Meter (ft³)**: Multiply `ft³` by **31.5** to convert to `kWh`.

### 3. The Two Pricing Components
- **Unit Rate Charge (p/kWh)**: Capped by Ofgem at ~6.2p/kWh for standard variable tariffs.
- **Daily Standing Charge (p/day)**: Fixed fee of ~31.4p/day (~£114.61/year) charged every day regardless of gas usage.

## 📊 Practical Gas Bill Worked Examples

Below are two worked calculation examples illustrating annual gas bill estimates:

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 1: Medium 3-bedroom house consuming 11,500 kWh/year (6.2p/kWh & 31.4p/day standing charge)</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Annual Gas Usage: **11,500 kWh**</li>

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Gas Bill Warnings

1. **Summer vs Winter Seasonal Variance**: Up to **80% of your annual gas usage occurs between October and March**. Direct debit payments smooth this across 12 equal monthly payments, creating temporary summer credit and winter debit balances.
2. **Boiler Thermostat & Radiator TRVs**: Turning your room thermostat down by just **1°C** (e.g. from 21°C to 20°C) reduces annual gas consumption by up to **10%**, saving over £80 per year.
3. **Calorific Value Fluctuations**: The 'Calorific Value' (CV) of mains gas measures energy density and varies slightly depending on gas supply source (usually between 38.0 and 41.0 MJ/m³).
