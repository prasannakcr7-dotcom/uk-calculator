Calculating how many **facing bricks** and **mortar materials (building sand and cement)** you need for a house extension, garden wall, garage, or cavity wall outer leaf is a fundamental masonry calculation.

In the UK, standard metric facing bricks measure **215mm long × 102.5mm wide × 65mm high**. Allowing for a standard **10mm mortar bed joint**, exactly **60 bricks are required per square metre of single-skin wall**.

## ⚙️ Statutory UK Brick Dimensions & Industry Rules for 2026/27

### 1. Standard UK Brick & Joint Dimensions (BS EN 771)
- **Work Dimensions (Individual Brick)**: **215mm Length × 102.5mm Width × 65mm Height**.
- **Coordinating Dimensions (With 10mm Mortar Joint)**: **225mm Length × 102.5mm Width × 75mm Height**.
- **Bricks Per Square Metre**:
  - **Single-Skin Half-Brick Wall (102.5mm thick)**: **60 bricks per sq m**.
  - **Double-Skin Solid Brick Wall (215mm thick)**: **120 bricks per sq m**.
  - **Cavity Wall Outer Leaf (102.5mm thick)**: **60 bricks per sq m**.

### 2. Brick Pack Quantities & Wastage Buffer
- UK facing bricks are manufactured and delivered in shrink-wrapped factory packs containing **400 to 500 bricks per pack** (typically 500 bricks per pallet).
- **Cutting & Damage Wastage**: Always add a **10% waste allowance** for broken bricks, half-brick cuts at corners, and window reveals.

### 3. Mortar Material Estimations (1:4 Cement : Sand Mix)
Laying 1,000 UK facing bricks requires approximately **1 tonne (1,000 kg) of bricklaying mortar**:
- **1,000 Bricks Require**:
  - **Plasticised Building Sand**: ~850 kg (1 Jumbo Bulk Bag).
  - **Ordinary Portland Cement**: ~6 to 7 bags of 25kg Cement (165 kg).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Brickwork Warnings

1. **Forgetting Window and Door Openings**: Subtract the surface area of double doors or windows from total wall elevation area before multiplying by 60 bricks/sq m.
2. **Mixing Different Brick Batch Packs**: Clay facing bricks vary in shade between kiln firings. Professional bricklayers 'draw' bricks from 3 or 4 different packs simultaneously to blend color variations evenly across the wall.
3. **Movement Joint Requirements**: Under BDA technical guidelines, long brick walls require vertical expansion movement joints (with flexible sealant and ties) every **10 to 12 metres** to prevent thermal cracking.

    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>A standard designation (iii) mortar mix consists of **1 part Cement : 1 part Lime : 5 to 6 parts Soft Building Sand** (or 1 part Cement to 4 parts Soft Sand with liquid plasticiser).</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is the difference between facing bricks and engineering bricks? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Facing bricks are decorative outer wall bricks. Engineering bricks (Class A or B) are dense, high-strength, low-absorption bricks used below damp proof course (DPC) level or in retaining walls.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

    <button class="faq-trigger">How high can a 102.5mm single-skin garden wall be built safely? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>

      <p>Under UK building standards, a single-skin (102.5mm) unreinforced freestanding garden wall should not exceed **450mm in height** unless built with piers (pillars) spaced every 3 metres.</p>
