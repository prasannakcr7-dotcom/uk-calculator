The **National Minimum Wage (NMW)** and **National Living Wage (NLW)** are the statutory minimum pay rates per hour that almost all UK workers are legally entitled to receive.

Employers who pay below these statutory minimum rates face severe HMRC penalties, public naming and shaming, and mandatory backpay orders.

## ⚙️ Statutory NMW & NLW Hourly Rates for 2026/27

### 1. Statutory Rate Brackets by Age
- **National Living Wage (Aged 21 and over)**: **£12.21 per hour**.
- **Youth Rate (Aged 18 to 20)**: **£10.00 per hour**.
- **Under 18 Rate (Aged 16 to 17)**: **£7.55 per hour**.
- **Apprentice Rate**: **£7.55 per hour** (for under 19s or 19+ in Year 1).

### 2. Standard Full-Time Minimum Salaries (37.5 Hours/Week)
- **Aged 21+ (NLW)**: £457.88 / week (**£23,809.50 per year**).
- **Aged 18 to 20**: £375.00 / week (**£19,500.00 per year**).
- **Aged 16 to 17 / Apprentice**: £283.13 / week (**£14,722.50 per year**).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & HMRC Compliance Warnings

1. **Unlawful Deductions Dropping Effective Pay Below NMW**: Deductions from your wage packet for work uniforms, security passes, or tools that drop your effective hourly pay below £12.21/hr are illegal breaches of NMW law.
2. **Unpaid Trial Shifts and Mandatory Training**: Employers MUST pay National Minimum Wage for all mandatory training sessions, team meetings, and working trial shifts.
3. **Failing to Upgrade Pay on Birthdays**: Employers must automatically increase an employee's hourly pay rate starting from the first pay reference period following their 18th or 21st birthday.

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Are piece rate workers covered by the National Minimum Wage? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Yes. Piece rate workers (paid per task or item produced) must be paid at least the National Minimum Wage per hour worked, or receive a statutory fair piece rate set at 1.2 times the average output rate.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Does the National Minimum Wage apply to self-employed freelancers? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No. Genuine self-employed freelancers and independent contractors are not covered by National Minimum Wage legislation. NMW applies strictly to workers and employees.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is the difference between the National Living Wage and the Real Living Wage? <span class="icon">▼</span></button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>The National Living Wage (£12.21/hr) is the mandatory statutory minimum set by the UK government. The Real Living Wage is an optional higher rate (calculated by the Living Wage Foundation based on actual living costs) voluntarily paid by accredited employers.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
