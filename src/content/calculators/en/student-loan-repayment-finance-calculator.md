UK Student Loans operate as a specialized **graduate tax** collected automatically through employer PAYE payroll by HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC).

Understanding how your specific **Student Loan Plan Type (Plan 1, Plan 2, Plan 5, or Postgraduate)** and statutory repayment threshold determine your monthly salary deduction ensures complete paycheck transparency.

## ⚙️ Statutory Student Loan Repayment Thresholds for 2026/27

### 1. Undergraduate Repayment Plans (9% Repayment Rate)
You pay **9% of your gross annual salary above the threshold** for your specific loan plan:
- **Plan 1 (Pre-2012 England/Wales; all NI & Scotland)**: Repayment threshold **£24,990 per year** (£2,082/month).
- **Plan 2 (2012 to 2023 England & Wales)**: Repayment threshold **£27,295 per year** (£2,274/month).
- **Plan 5 (Post-August 2023 England)**: Repayment threshold **£25,000 per year** (£2,083/month).

### 2. Postgraduate Masters & Doctoral Loans (6% Repayment Rate)
- **Postgraduate Plan**: Repayment threshold **£21,000 per year** (£1,750/month). You pay **6% of your gross annual salary above £21,000**.
- **Combined Repayments**: If you hold both an undergraduate loan (Plan 2) and a Postgraduate loan, you repay **15% total** (9% Plan 2 + 6% PG) on earnings above £27,295.

## 📊 Practical Student Loan Worked Examples

Below are two worked calculation examples illustrating monthly student loan deductions:

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 1: Graduate on a £38,000 salary with a Plan 2 Student Loan</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Gross Annual Salary: **£38,000.00**</li>
    <li>Plan 2 Repayment Threshold: **£27,295.00**</li>
    <li>Assessable Salary above Threshold: £38,000 - £27,295 = **£10,705.00**</li>
    <li>Annual Repayment (9% of £10,705): **£963.45 / year**</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Calculation: Monthly PAYE deduction = £963.45 ÷ 12 = £80.28 per month.</p>
  <div class="net-highlight">Monthly Student Loan Deduction: **£80.28 / month** (£963.45 per year)</div>
</div>

<div class="example-box">
  <strong>Example 2: Graduate on a £45,000 salary with both Plan 2 and Postgraduate loans</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Gross Annual Salary: **£45,000.00**</li>
    <li>Plan 2 Repayment (9% above £27,295): (£45,000 - £27,295) × 9% = **£1,593.45 / year** (£132.79/mo)</li>
    <li>Postgraduate Repayment (6% above £21,000): (£45,000 - £21,000) × 6% = **£1,440.00 / year** (£120.00/mo)</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Calculation: Total monthly PAYE deduction = £132.79 + £120.00 = £252.79 per month.</p>
  <div class="net-highlight">Total Monthly Student Loan Deduction: **£252.79 / month** (£3,033.45 per year)</div>

## 📑 Common Pitfalls & Student Loan Warnings

1. **Overpaying via Bonus or Overtime Month**: Student loan PAYE deductions are calculated on a **per-pay-period basis**. If you receive an annual bonus or overtime payment in a single month, HMRC deducts 9% on that month's elevated pay, even if your total annual salary ends up below the threshold.
2. **Voluntary Overpayments Risk**: Making voluntary extra repayments to clear a Plan 2 or Plan 5 loan early is usually financially unadvisable for most graduates. Because unpaid balances are cancelled after 30 to 40 years, overpaying cash that will eventually be written off wastes personal savings.
3. **Self Assessment Unearned Income Trap**: If you file a Self Assessment tax return and earn more than £2,000 in unearned income (such as dividends or rental profits), student loan repayments apply to your total combined income.
