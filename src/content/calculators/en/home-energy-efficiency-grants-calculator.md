To achieve net-zero targets and lower household energy bills, UK national and devolved governments provide substantial financial grants, subsidies, and tax incentives to help homeowners replace fossil fuel boilers with low-carbon heating (such as heat pumps) and upgrade home insulation.

Because energy policy is partially devolved, the specific grant programs and maximum funding amounts vary depending on whether your home is located in **England & Wales**, **Scotland**, or **Northern Ireland**.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS - England & Wales)**: Provides an upfront **£7,500 voucher** towards replacing fossil fuel boilers (gas, oil, LPG) with an Air Source Heat Pump (ASHP) or Ground Source Heat Pump (GSHP).
- **Home Energy Scotland Scheme (Scotland)**: Offers a **£7,500 grant** (£9,000 for eligible rural or low-income households) plus optional interest-free loans for heat pumps and insulation.
- **ECO4 & Great British Insulation Scheme (GBIS)**: Provides **100% free loft, cavity wall, or solid wall insulation** for low-income households receiving means-tested benefits (Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Income Support).
- **Statutory 0% VAT Rate**: All residential installations of energy-saving materials (solar PV, heat pumps, insulation) in the UK benefit from **0% VAT until 31 March 2027**, saving 20% compared to standard VAT.

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Using Non-MCS Certified Installers**: Grants (including BUS and Home Energy Scotland) require installation by Microgeneration Certification Scheme (MCS) certified installers; uncertified work forfeits the £7,500 grant.
- **Forgetting Insulation Prerequisites**: Heat pumps require adequate home insulation to operate at maximum efficiency; check whether your EPC has outstanding cavity wall or loft insulation recommendations.
- **Replacing Existing Heat Pumps**: BUS vouchers only apply when replacing fossil fuel heating systems (gas, oil, direct electric); replacing an existing heat pump does not qualify.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<div class="faq-sec">
  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How do I apply for the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>You do not apply for the BUS voucher directly yourself. Your chosen MCS-certified installer submits the application to Ofgem on your behalf. The £7,500 grant discount is automatically deducted directly from your final installation invoice.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Who qualifies for free insulation under the ECO4 scheme?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>ECO4 provides fully funded insulation and heating upgrades to homeowners and private tenants living in energy-inefficient properties (EPC rating D, E, F, or G) where a household member receives eligible means-tested benefits such as Universal Credit, Pension Credit, or Child Tax Credit.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">How does the 0% VAT rating on energy-saving materials work?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Until 31 March 2027, the UK government has reduced VAT to 0% on the supply and installation of energy-saving materials (including solar panels, heat pumps, insulation, and battery storage) in residential properties. Your installer automatically applies 0% VAT to your bill.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Are heat pump grants available for new-build homes?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>No. Standard new-build properties are excluded from the Boiler Upgrade Scheme because modern building regulations mandate efficient low-carbon heating. However, self-build homes built by individuals for their own use are eligible for the BUS grant.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
