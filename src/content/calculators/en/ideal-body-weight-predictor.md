Predicting your **Ideal Body Weight (IBW)** provides a realistic, healthy target range for weight loss, fitness goals, and medical dosage calculations.

In clinical medicine, **Ideal Body Weight** is calculated using established mathematical formulas (such as the **Devine Formula**, **Robinson Formula**, and **Miller Formula**) that base optimal weight primarily on biological gender and height above 5 feet (152.4 cm). In addition, the **NHS** defines a healthy weight range as any weight that yields a **BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 kg/m²**.

## ⚙️ Rules & Thresholds

- **Devine Formula (Clinical Gold Standard)**:
  - **Men**: `50 kg + 2.3 kg for every inch of height over 5 feet`.
  - **Women**: `45.5 kg + 2.3 kg for every inch of height over 5 feet`.
- **NHS Healthy Weight Range Formula**:
  - `Minimum Healthy Weight (kg) = 18.5 × (Height in metres)²`.
  - `Maximum Healthy Weight (kg) = 24.9 × (Height in metres)²`.
- **Height Threshold**: Clinical IBW formulas assume a minimum baseline height of 5 feet (60 inches / 152.4 cm).

## 📑 Common Pitfalls

- **Confusing a Single Ideal Weight with a Rigid Requirement**: Health is a range; any weight within the NHS healthy BMI band (BMI 18.5 to 24.9) represents a healthy weight.
- **Ignoring Frame Size and Muscle Density**: Individuals with naturally broad skeletal frames or high muscle mass can sit comfortably above theoretical IBW targets without excess body fat.
- **Setting Unrealistic Rapid Weight Loss Goals**: Attempting to reach an ideal weight too quickly (> 1 kg/week) causes muscle wasting and metabolic slowdown.

    <button class="faq-trigger">What is the Devine formula for Ideal Body Weight?</button>

      <p>The Devine formula is a medical equation developed in 1974 by Dr. Ben J. Devine to estimate ideal body weight for drug dosage calculations. It sets a baseline weight for a 5-foot person (50 kg for men, 45.5 kg for women) and adds 2.3 kg per inch above 5 feet.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">What is the NHS healthy weight range for my height?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>The NHS defines a healthy weight range for any adult as a weight that produces a Body Mass Index (BMI) between 18.5 and 24.9 kg/m². For example, for a person 175 cm tall, the healthy weight range is 56.7 kg to 76.3 kg.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <button class="faq-trigger">Does ideal body weight change with age?</button>
    <div class="faq-content">
      <p>Standard IBW formulas do not adjust for age. However, research suggests that slightly higher BMI ranges (BMI 23 to 27) in older adults over age 65 are associated with better health resilience and protection against illness.</p>
    </div>
